# Epics that never close are areas, not projects

A backlog full of permanent epics, such as "Onboarding improvements" or "Card fraud", is Forte's list of areas under another name. It hides how much work is committed and never shows progress. Breaking each standing epic into dated, closable outcomes would make capacity visible to stakeholders and give the team a steady cadence of finished work.

## Why I think this
- Forte: a list of areas doesn't show the size of the workload. "Hiring" could mean one hire or fifty ([[raw/forte-para-method.md]]).
- Forte: areas never turn over, which kills motivation. Breaking them into projects creates "a cadence of regular victories" ([[raw/forte-para-method.md]]).
- The mapping to backlogs and epics is my extension to product work. The source is about personal information, not delivery tooling.
