# Bush linked documents, evergreen notes link ideas

Bush's trails join items already on the record, such as a page of a book, so a link points at a whole document. Matuschak's atomic notes shrink the unit to one idea you wrote yourself, so a link says which ideas connect, not just which documents. The compiled wiki sits in between: its entity and concept pages are finer than a document but hold many claims each.

## Why I think this
- The memex joins two items shown on its viewing positions ([[raw/bush-as-we-may-think.pdf#page=16]]).
- Evergreen notes should be atomic and densely linked ([[raw/matuschak-evergreen-notes.md]]).
- The compiled wiki's units are summaries, entity pages and concept pages ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).
- Open: the hub page doesn't define "atomic", so this rests on the principle's title until that note is clipped.
