# Payment and e-money complaints need their own workflow, because their clock runs in business days and the Ombudsman's door opens the day it is missed

A bank can't run payment and e-money complaints on its eight-week workflow with a shorter due date. The rules differ in kind as well as in length. The clock counts business days. A delay needs a holding response that gives reasons and a new deadline, and that response has to go out inside the first window. When the clock runs out, the customer can go straight to the Ombudsman. So the case system has to classify a complaint as PSD or EMD the day it arrives, and it needs its own branch for the holding response.

## Why I think this
- For EMD and PSD complaints, a final response is due "by the end of 15 business days after the day on which it received the complaint". In exceptional circumstances, the firm must send a holding response within 15 business days "clearly indicating the reasons for the delay" and the deadline, then a final response within 35 business days ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR).
- The final response for these complaints must be on paper, or on another durable medium if agreed ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR).
- The Ombudsman can consider an EMD or PSD complaint once 15 business days have passed with no holding response, or 35 business days after one ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R(2A)).
- In a mixed complaint, the payment part must follow the short rule. The firm may put the whole complaint on it, and must tell the customer if it deals with the complaint in parts ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]).
- Every other complaint has eight weeks ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R). So the class of the complaint, not its subject, sets the clock, and it has to be decided at intake (reasoning).
- The FOS tells consumers "15 days" and includes "fraud and scams" ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]). Customers may therefore expect an answer sooner than DISP requires, or expect the short clock on fraud complaints that aren't PSD complaints. This is contested, conflicts 1 and 2 of [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] (reasoning).
