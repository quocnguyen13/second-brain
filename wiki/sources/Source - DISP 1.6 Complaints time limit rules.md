# Source - DISP 1.6 Complaints time limit rules

**Raw file:** [[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]] · **Author or publisher:** Financial Conduct Authority, FCA Handbook (DISP 1.6, "Complaints time limit rules") · **Published:** last updated 2026-06-01; a future version is dated 2027-10-25; viewed as of 2026-10-14, the clip date · **Trust:** primary (a regulator's own rules) · **Ingested:** 2026-10-14

## Summary
These are the FCA Handbook rules on what a firm must do once a complaint arrives. It must acknowledge the complaint promptly, keep the complainant informed, and answer within eight weeks. Payment-services and e-money complaints must be answered within 15 business days, or 35 in exceptional circumstances. The rules set out what a final response, or a holding response, must contain, and that the FOS information must be prominent ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Complaint handling time limits]] and [[wiki/concepts/Final response]].

## Key claims

### Deadlines (emphasis)
- **On receipt.** The firm must send a prompt written acknowledgement saying it has the complaint and is dealing with it, and stating when it will respond. It must then keep the complainant informed of progress ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.1R).
- **Eight weeks.** For most complaints, the firm must send a final response, or a written response explaining the delay, by the end of eight weeks after receipt ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R).
- **Payment services and e-money** ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR):
  - **Normal deadline.** A final response by the end of 15 business days after the day of receipt.
  - **Exceptional circumstances.** If reasons beyond the firm's control prevent that, it must send a holding response within 15 business days giving the reasons and the deadline, then a final response within 35 business days.
  - **Format.** The final response must be on paper, or on another durable medium if agreed.
- **Mixed complaints.** The payment-services part of a mixed complaint follows the 15-day rule. Because that limit is shorter, a firm may choose to treat the whole complaint under it, and must tell the complainant if it deals with the complaint in parts ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]).
- **Written acceptance.** The eight-week rule does not apply if the complainant has already accepted a response in writing, provided that response gave the FOS information. For payment-services and e-money complaints, a final response must always be sent unless DISP 1.5.1R applies ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.4R).
- **Speed and quality.** Almost all complaints are expected to be substantively addressed within eight weeks. The FCA may judge a firm's response on its quality as well as its speed ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], Speed and quality of response).

### What a final response must contain (emphasis)
- **The decision.** It must accept the complaint, with redress where appropriate; or offer redress without accepting it; or reject it with reasons ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1)).
- **The FOS information.** It must ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1)(d)–(f)):
  - enclose the FOS's standard explanatory leaflet;
  - give the FOS website address;
  - tell the complainant they may now refer the complaint to the FOS;
  - say whether the firm consents to waive the DISP 2.8 time limits, using the wording in DISP 1 Annex 3R.
- **A delay response.** The written response sent at eight weeks without a final answer must explain why and say when the firm expects to answer. It must also say the complainant may now go to the FOS and whether the firm would waive the time limits, and it must enclose the leaflet and give the website address ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(2)).
- **Prominence.** The FOS information must be set out "clearly, comprehensibly, in an easily accessible way and prominently" within the text ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]).
- **Pensions Ombudsman.** Where relevant, a firm may also refer to the Pensions Ombudsman, using DISP 1 Annex 4G wording ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]).

## Conflicts and open points
- **Holding responses are unnamed.** The eight-week rule doesn't call its delay letter a "holding response". The payment-services rule does.
- **A future version.** A future version is dated 2027-10-25. Its content isn't in the clip.
- **15 days or 15 business days (fact).** The FOS's consumer page says "only 15 days" ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]), where this section says 15 business days, or 35 in exceptional circumstances. Resolved 2026-10-14, [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] conflict 1: this section's figure is stated, and the FOS's figure is marked outweighed, as this is the FCA's own statement. See [[wiki/concepts/Complaint handling time limits]].
- **Fraud and scams (fact).** The FOS page puts "fraud and scams" on the 15-day clock ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]). This section applies the short deadline to EMD and PSD complaints only. Resolved 2026-10-14, [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] conflict 2: this section's scope is stated, and the FOS's wording is marked outweighed, as this is the FCA's own statement.
