The PRA's landing page on the Bank of England site. Most of it is links (authorisation, supervision, FSCS, resolution, ring-fencing, Strong and Simple, stress testing, the New Bank Start-up Unit). Three short paragraphs say who the PRA is, how many firms it regulates, and what its rules and supervision are for ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]). It is the PRA's own count of its firm population.

## Key claims
- **Through the PRA.** The Bank of England prudentially regulates and supervises financial services firms through the PRA ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]).
- **Firm count.** As part of the Bank, the PRA is responsible for the prudential regulation and supervision of "around 1,292 banks, building societies, credit unions, insurers and major investment firms" ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]). The page was last updated 2026-09-21 ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]). See Conflicts below.
- **Rules.** It creates policy enacted through the PRA Rulebook; its rules require firms to keep sufficient capital and adequate risk controls ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]).
- **Supervision.** It supervises firms to keep a comprehensive overview of their activities, so it can step in if a firm is not run safely and soundly or, for insurers, is not protecting policyholders adequately ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]).
- **EU withdrawal.** Pre-2021 PRA supervisory statements and statements of policy should be read with SS1/19 on interpreting EU references ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]).
