# Source - FSMA 2000 (2026-09) - Part 4A

**Raw file:** [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf]] · **Author or publisher:** UK Parliament, via legislation.gov.uk · **Published:** Act of 14 June 2000; revised text up to date to 10 September 2026 · **Trust:** primary (legislation) · **Ingested:** 2026-10-20
**Part of:** [[wiki/sources/Source - FSMA 2000 (2026-09)]] · **Pages:** 82–119 · **Sections:** 55A–55Z4

## Summary
Part 4A, "Permission to carry on regulated activities", is how a firm becomes able to do what Part II forbids the unauthorised to do. A firm applies to "the appropriate regulator": the PRA when the activities include a PRA-regulated activity, the FCA otherwise ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(1)–(2)). The FCA gives permission on its own (s. 55E); the PRA only with the FCA's consent (s. 55F) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], ss. 55E(2), 55F(2)). Either way, the regulator must ensure the firm will meet, and keep meeting, the threshold conditions it is responsible for ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(3)). The Part then sets how a permission is varied or cancelled at the firm's request (ss. 55H–55I) or on the regulator's own initiative (ss. 55J–55K), how requirements are imposed with it (ss. 55L–55N), and a separate FCA permission to approve other people's financial promotions (ss. 55NA–55NB) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55J; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA). Last come procedure and challenge: what an application contains, a 6-month deadline from a complete application (12 months at most for an incomplete one), warning and decision notices, and a right to refer to the Tribunal ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=108]], s. 55U; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(1)–(2); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=111]], s. 55X; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=118]], s. 55Z3). See [[wiki/concepts/Part 4A permission]].

## Key claims

### Applying for permission (ss. 55A, 55G) (emphasis)
- **Who may apply.** An individual, a body corporate, a partnership or an unincorporated association may apply to the appropriate regulator for permission to carry on one or more regulated activities ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(1)).
- **Which regulator.** The PRA, where the activities applied for "consist of or include a PRA-regulated activity", or where the applicant is already a PRA-authorised person otherwise than by a Part 4A permission; the FCA in any other case ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(2)). Administering a benchmark is always the FCA's, and must be applied for on its own ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(2A)–(2B)).
- **One permission per firm.** An authorised person with a Part 4A permission in force may not apply for permission under s. 55A ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(3)); it applies to vary the one it has (ss. 55H–55I, below).
- **The name.** A permission given under this Part is "a Part 4A permission" ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(5)). It does not include permission to approve financial promotions under s. 55NA ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(6)).
- **Special cases (light pass).** An applicant already exempt for one activity is treated, depending on the source of the exemption, as applying for all the activities it will carry on, or only for the new one ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=87]], s. 55G(2)–(4)). Where an application resembles one the other regulator refused or treated as invalid, the regulator must have regard to minimising the applicant's extra work and the time taken ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55G(5)–(6)).

### The threshold conditions (ss. 55B–55D)
- **What they are.** The conditions set out in or under Schedule 6, read with any threshold condition code either regulator makes under s. 137O ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(1)). Each regulator is responsible for those Schedule 6 conditions expressed to be relevant to its functions ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(2)). See [[wiki/concepts/Threshold Conditions]].
- **The duty (emphasis).** In giving or varying permission, imposing or varying a requirement, or giving consent under this Part, each regulator must ensure that the person "will satisfy, and continue to satisfy", for all the regulated activities it has or will have permission for, the threshold conditions that regulator is responsible for ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(3)).
- **Not the only test.** The duty doesn't stop a regulator, with due regard to it, taking the steps it considers necessary in relation to a particular person to advance, for the FCA, its operational objectives, and for the PRA, any of its objectives ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(4)). It doesn't apply to administering a benchmark ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(5)).
- **An exclusion in force.** Section 55B(3) is excluded for specified purposes from 15 July 2025, and from 15 July 2026 so far as not already in force, by the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (Regulated Activities etc.) (Amendment) Order 2025 (S.I. 2025/859), art. 11(6) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=85]], C149). The annotation doesn't say which purposes; the order is not in `raw/`.
- **Changing them.** The Treasury may amend Parts 1 and 2 of Schedule 6 by order, with different provision for each regulator, for different activities, and for PRA-regulated and other firms ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=85]], s. 55C).
- **Non-UK firms.** In judging whether a non-UK firm meets the threshold conditions, a UK regulator may have regard to an opinion from the firm's home regulator, weighted by the nature and scope of that regulator's supervision ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=85]], s. 55D(2)–(4)).

### Giving permission (ss. 55E–55F) (emphasis)
- **The FCA.** Where it is the appropriate regulator, the FCA may give permission for the activities applied for, or some of them ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], s. 55E(1)–(2)). It must consult the PRA first if the applicant is in a group that includes a PRA-authorised person ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], s. 55E(3)).
- **The PRA.** Where it is the appropriate regulator, the PRA may give permission "with the consent of the FCA" ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], s. 55F(2)). The FCA's consent may be conditional on how the PRA words the permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=87]], s. 55F(5)). The PRA can't give a permission that relates only to activities that aren't PRA-regulated, unless the applicant is already PRA-authorised otherwise than by a Part 4A permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=87]], s. 55F(6)).
- **What a permission says.** Either regulator must specify the permitted regulated activities, described as it considers appropriate. It may build limitations into the description (for example, circumstances in which the activity may or may not be carried on), describe an activity more narrowly or widely than applied for, and give permission for an activity not applied for. The FCA may add only activities that aren't PRA-regulated ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], s. 55E(4)–(5); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=87]], s. 55F(3)–(4)).
- **Product-owner reading.** A permission is a list of described activities, possibly with limitations. Whether a new product is covered depends on that wording, not only on the kind of activity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], s. 55E(4)–(5); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=87]], s. 55F(3)–(4)). Acting outside it breaches a requirement ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=52]], s. 20(1), (1A)); see [[wiki/sources/Source - FSMA 2000 (2026-09) - Part II]].

### Varying or cancelling at the firm's request (ss. 55H–55I) (emphasis)
- **The FCA, for FCA-only firms.** On the application of an authorised person that has a Part 4A permission and isn't PRA-authorised, the FCA may vary the permission by adding a regulated activity other than a PRA-regulated one, removing an activity, or varying an activity's description; and it may cancel the permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(2)–(3)). For a PRA-authorised person, the FCA varies only the activity of administering a benchmark, after consulting the PRA ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(3A)–(3B)).
- **FCA grounds to refuse.** It may refuse if that is desirable to advance any of its operational objectives, or the purpose of its Part 8B functions ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(4)). It must consult the PRA first if the applicant is in a group that includes a PRA-authorised person ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55H(5)).
- **The PRA, for PRA-authorised firms.** On the application of a PRA-authorised person, the PRA may, with the FCA's consent, add an activity, remove one, or vary a description ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I(1)). It may cancel the permission after consulting the FCA ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I(2)).
- **Becoming dual-regulated.** On the application of an authorised person that isn't PRA-authorised, the PRA, with the FCA's consent, may add activities that include a PRA-regulated activity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(3)). See [[wiki/concepts/Solo and dual regulation]].
- **Refusal and consent.** The PRA may refuse to advance any of its objectives; the FCA may withhold consent to advance one or more of its operational objectives; its consent may be conditional on how the PRA words the varied permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(4)–(5), (8)).
- **Empty permissions.** If a variation leaves no activities, the regulator must cancel the permission once satisfied it is no longer necessary to keep it in force (the PRA after consulting the FCA) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55H(6); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(6)).
- **A variation can do what a grant can.** It may include any provision a fresh permission could ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55H(7); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(7)).

### Varying or cancelling on the regulator's initiative (ss. 55J–55KA) (emphasis)
- **Grounds.** Either regulator may act if the firm is failing, or likely to fail, the threshold conditions it is responsible for; has not carried on a permitted activity for at least 12 months; or if acting is desirable to advance the FCA's operational objectives or any of the PRA's objectives. The FCA may also act on breaches of Part 5 of the Alternative Investment Fund Managers Regulations 2013 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55J(1)).
- **The FCA's power.** To vary by adding an activity other than a PRA-regulated one, removing an activity, or varying a description (not, for a PRA-regulated activity, so as to widen it); or to cancel ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(2)).
- **The PRA's power.** For a PRA-authorised person, to vary in any way in s. 55I(1), or cancel; for another authorised person, to add a PRA-regulated activity and then vary in those ways ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(3)).
- **Consent runs both ways here.** The FCA must consult the PRA before acting on a PRA-authorised person or a member of its group, and needs the PRA's consent to add an activity to, or widen a description in, a PRA-authorised person's permission. The PRA must consult the FCA before acting, and needs the FCA's consent to add an activity or widen a description ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(4)–(5)). Removing an activity or cancelling on its own initiative needs only consultation ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(5)(a); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=94]], s. 55J(9)).
- **Sector grounds (light pass).** Further grounds to cancel or vary apply to investment firms (s. 55K: at least 6 months' inactivity, a false statement, no longer meeting the authorisation requirements, serious and systematic infringement), full-scope UK AIFMs, mortgage intermediaries (at least 6 months without a relevant mortgage activity, a false statement, no longer meeting the conditions, serious or systematic infringement of their operating conditions), UK UCITS managers, investment firms breaching the market abuse regulation, and benchmark administrators ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(6)–(6A); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=92]], s. 55J(6B); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=93]], s. 55J(6C), (7ZA)–(7ZC); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=96]], s. 55K(1)). Subsections (7A)–(7C) were repealed on 14 May 2025 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=95]], F271).
- **Empty permissions and naming.** A variation that leaves no activities must lead to cancellation, after consulting the other regulator if the firm was PRA-authorised ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=94]], s. 55J(8)–(9)). The power under s. 55J is the regulator's "own-initiative variation power" ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=94]], s. 55J(12)).
- **An extra FCA power.** Schedule 6A gives the FCA an additional power to vary or cancel the Part 4A permission of a firm that isn't PRA-authorised ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=96]], s. 55JA), inserted from 1 July 2021 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=96]], F273). Schedule 6A is not compiled.
- **Repealed.** Section 55KA (insurers) was repealed on 14 May 2025 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], F278).

### Requirements (ss. 55L–55N) (emphasis)
- **With a permission.** Where a person applies, to either regulator, for a Part 4A permission or a variation, the FCA may impose requirements taking effect on or after the grant or variation ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L(1)). The PRA may do the same where the application involves a PRA-regulated activity or comes from a PRA-authorised person ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=98]], s. 55M(1)).
- **On their own initiative.** On the same three grounds as s. 55J (threshold conditions, 12 months' inactivity, objectives), the FCA may impose, vary or cancel a requirement on any firm with a Part 4A permission, and the PRA on a PRA-authorised person. This is each regulator's "own-initiative requirement power" ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L(2)–(4); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=98]], s. 55M(2)–(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=99]], s. 55M(4)).
- **At the firm's request.** Either regulator may impose, vary or cancel a requirement on the firm's application, and may refuse to advance its objectives ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L(5)–(6); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=99]], s. 55M(5)–(6)).
- **Consultation.** The FCA must consult the PRA before imposing or varying a requirement on a PRA-authorised person, a future one, or a member of its group ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=98]], s. 55L(7)). The PRA must consult the FCA before imposing or varying any requirement ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=99]], s. 55M(7)).
- **What a requirement can do.** Require the firm to take, or refrain from, specified action; extend to activities that aren't regulated; be set by reference to the firm's group; expire after a set period; and refer to past conduct, for example by requiring the firm to review, or take remedial action in respect of, past conduct ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=99]], s. 55N(1)–(5)).

### Approving financial promotions (ss. 55NA–55NB) (emphasis)
- **The rule.** An authorised person must not approve the content of a communication for s. 21 unless the FCA has given it permission to do so under s. 55NA. Approving outside that permission counts as breaching a requirement imposed by the FCA ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(1)–(2)). See [[wiki/concepts/Financial promotion]].
- **Applying.** An authorised person, or an applicant for Part 4A permission whose application is not yet decided, may apply ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(3)). The FCA may grant it on the terms sought, which may be a general permission to approve, or on other terms, such as a narrower description ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(4)), and must set out the terms ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(6)).
- **Refusal, variation, cancellation.** The FCA may refuse an application to advance its operational objectives ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(7)). On its own initiative it may vary or cancel the permission if the firm has for at least 12 months failed to give, or refuse to give, any approvals in line with it, or to advance its operational objectives ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(5), (8)).
- **Consultation.** The FCA must consult the PRA for a PRA-authorised person (present or future) or a member of its group, and the Gibraltar regulator for a Gibraltar-based person ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(9); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=101]], s. 55NA(9)–(10)).
- **Exemptions.** The rule doesn't apply within an exemption in Treasury regulations under s. 55NB, which may cover specified persons or classes, activities, circumstances, or conditions ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=101]], ss. 55NA(11), 55NB(1)–(2)). Other requirements on approvals still apply, but any inconsistent with s. 55NA(1) has no effect ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=101]], s. 55NA(12)).
- **In force.** Inserted by the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023, in stages from 29 June 2023 and fully from 7 February 2024 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=101]], F281). Section 55NA(1) is excluded by the Exemptions from Financial Promotion General Requirement Regulations 2023 from 27 September 2023, and temporarily by the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2025 from 19 January 2026 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=101]], C165, C166).

### Further powers (ss. 55O–55T) (light pass)
- **Acquisition of control.** Where someone acquires control of a UK authorised person with a Part 4A permission, there are no grounds for the own-initiative requirement power, and the likely effect is uncertain, the appropriate regulator may impose or vary a requirement it could have imposed on giving permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=102]], s. 55O(1)–(2)). For a PRA-authorised person that is the FCA or the PRA; otherwise the FCA. Duties to consult or obtain consent still apply ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=102]], s. 55O(3)–(4)).
- **Assets requirements.** A requirement under s. 55L or 55M may prohibit or restrict dealing with the firm's assets, or require its assets, or consumers' assets it holds, to be transferred to an approved trustee ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=103]], s. 55P(4)). A bank given notice of such a requirement doesn't breach its contract with the firm by refusing a payment it reasonably believes would breach it, and is liable to the regulator for a payment made in breach ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=103]], s. 55P(5)–(6)).
- **General meetings.** Where a bank, building society or investment firm doesn't comply with a requirement to call a general meeting, and has infringed or is likely soon to infringe certain requirements, the regulator may call the meeting itself and propose business ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=104]], s. 55PB(1)–(4)).
- **For an overseas regulator.** Either regulator's own-initiative powers may be used at the request of, or to assist, an overseas regulator of a prescribed kind; the duties to consult or obtain consent still apply ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=105]], s. 55Q(1), (3)). In deciding, it may weigh reciprocity, whether the breach has a UK parallel, seriousness and the public interest, and may ask for a contribution to costs ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=105]], s. 55Q(5)–(6)).
- **Connected persons.** In deciding on an application, a variation or cancellation, a requirement or a consent under this Part, a regulator may have regard to anyone in a relevant relationship with the applicant or permission holder ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=106]], s. 55R(1)).
- **Whose interests.** For the objectives tests in this Part, it doesn't matter whether the person a power is used against has any relationship with the people it protects ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=107]], s. 55T).
- **Omitted.** Sections 55PA, 55S and 55Z1–55Z2A were omitted from 31 December 2020 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=104]], F285; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=107]], F296; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=117]], F316).

### Applying and the deadlines (ss. 55U–55W) (emphasis)
- **What an application contains.** For a permission: the regulated activities the applicant proposes to carry on, and a UK address for service. For a variation: the desired variation and the activities the applicant will carry on if it is granted ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=108]], s. 55U(1)–(2)). It must be made as the regulator directs, with the information it reasonably requires ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=108]], s. 55U(4)).
- **Further information.** Until the application is decided, the regulator may ask for further information it reasonably considers necessary; for an application to the PRA, either the PRA or the FCA may ask ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=108]], s. 55U(5)–(6)). The PRA must consult the FCA before giving a direction or imposing a requirement for a class of applications ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=109]], s. 55U(9)).
- **Six months from a complete application.** An application under this Part must be decided within 6 months of the date the regulator received the completed application ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(1)).
- **Twelve months at most if incomplete.** The regulator may decide an incomplete application, and must decide it within 12 months of receiving it ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(2)).
- **Consent inside the same clock.** Where the decision needs the other regulator's consent, that regulator must decide within the same period ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(3)).
- **Exceptions.** An application for a Part 4A permission, or a variation, relating only to insurance distribution activity has 3 months instead of 6 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(9)). An application that would make the applicant a full-scope UK AIFM follows the AIFM Regulations 2013 instead of subsections (1)–(3) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(8)). Section 55V(1) is also excluded or modified for specified purposes by several orders ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=111]], C182–C186); the orders are not in `raw/`.
- **Withdrawal and notice.** The applicant may withdraw by written notice before the decision ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(4)). A grant is notified in writing, stating the date it has effect; a PRA notice granting a permission or a variation must state that the FCA has consented ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(5)–(7)).
- **The PRA tells the FCA.** As soon as practicable, the PRA notifies the FCA when it receives, or the applicant withdraws, an application for permission, for a variation under s. 55I, or about a requirement under s. 55M(5) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=111]], s. 55W).
- **What the Act doesn't say.** These are statutory maximums. The Act sets no typical duration for a decision.

### Notices, procedure and the Tribunal (ss. 55X–55Z4) (light pass)
- **Warning and decision notices on applications.** A regulator that proposes to refuse an application, or to grant it on narrower or different terms than asked (by limiting or re-describing the activities, or adding requirements), must give a warning notice; when it so decides, a decision notice ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=111]], s. 55X(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=112]], s. 55X(2), (4)). Benchmark applications to the FCA follow a written-notice and review route instead ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=112]], s. 55X(5); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=113]], s. 55XA).
- **Own-initiative action.** A variation or requirement takes effect immediately, on a date set, or when no longer open to review. It may take effect immediately or on a set date only if the regulator reasonably considers that necessary, given its ground ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=114]], s. 55Y(2)–(3)). The firm gets written notice with details, reasons, when it takes effect, and its right to make representations and to refer to the Tribunal; a further notice follows the regulator's decision on the representations ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=115]], s. 55Y(4)–(9)).
- **Cancellation.** Cancelling a Part 4A permission, or a s. 55NA permission, other than at the firm's request needs a warning notice and then a decision notice ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=116]], s. 55Z(1)–(2)).
- **The Tribunal.** An applicant aggrieved by the determination of an application under this Part, or a firm aggrieved by either regulator's own-initiative variation or requirement power, or by the FCA's s. 55NA(5)(b) power, may refer the matter to the Tribunal ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=118]], s. 55Z3(1)–(2A)).
- **Interpretation.** Section 55Z4 points the terms "own-initiative requirement power" and "own-initiative variation power" to ss. 55L(4), 55M(4) and 55J(12) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=119]], s. 55Z4). Part V begins on the same page ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=119]]).

## Conflicts and open points
- **The MoU on consent (scope).** The MoU says that, as FSMA provides, the PRA obtains the FCA's consent before granting or removing a permission or adding an activity, and consults it before cancelling ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=21]], para 1). That matches the Act when a firm applies (ss. 55F(2), 55I(1)–(2)). On the PRA's own initiative, removing an activity needs only consultation (s. 55J(5)). Resolved 2026-10-20 as scope (c), [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-20]] conflict 1: [[wiki/concepts/FCA-PRA coordination]] now states the application route (Act and MoU) and the own-initiative route (Act) separately.
- **Prospective text.** None in pp. 82–119.
- **How long a variation takes.** The wiki holds an AI-only claim that a variation can take months, on [[wiki/sources/Source - Claude on who regulates what (2026-09-27)]]. Section 55V sets only the maximums: 6 months from a complete application, 12 months for an incomplete one ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(1)–(2)). It doesn't say how long decisions usually take, so the claim stays `· AI`.
- **Not compiled.** Schedule 6 (the threshold conditions themselves) is plan 5; Schedule 6A (the FCA's additional power) is not in the plan; S.I. 2025/859, which excludes s. 55B(3) for specified purposes, is not in `raw/`.
