# Source - DISP 3.7 Ombudsman awards

**Raw file:** [[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]] · **Author or publisher:** Financial Conduct Authority, FCA Handbook (DISP 3.7, "Awards by the Ombudsman") · **Published:** last updated 2026-03-26; viewed as of 2026-10-14, the clip date · **Trust:** primary (a regulator's own rules) · **Ingested:** 2026-10-14

## Summary
These are the FCA Handbook rules on what the Ombudsman can award when it upholds a complaint: money, interest, costs and directions. Money awards are capped, and the cap rises with CPI each April. Firms must comply promptly, and complainants can enforce awards in the courts ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Ombudsman awards]].

## Key claims
- **Four kinds of award.** When a complaint is upheld, the determination may include a money award, an interest award, a costs award, a direction, or a combination ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
- **What money covers.** Fair compensation for financial loss (including consequential or prospective loss), pain and suffering, damage to reputation, and distress or inconvenience, "whether or not a court would award compensation". For a "relevant complaint" under s. 404B(3), it is instead the amount the firm should pay under the scheme ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Money awards).
- **The cap** ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Money awards):
  - **Base amounts.** £350,000 for an act or omission on or after 1 April 2019, and £160,000 for one before that date.
  - **Indexation.** Both rise each 1 April by CPI since January 2019, rounded down to the nearest £5,000. Each year's figures apply to complaints referred from that April to the next 31 March.
- **Current caps.** For complaints referred on or after 1 April 2026, the cap is £455,000 for acts on or after 1 April 2019, and £205,000 for earlier acts ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Note table). Earlier years:

  | Referred on or after | Act before 1 April 2019 | Act on or after 1 April 2019 |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 April 2019 | £160,000 | £350,000 |
  | 1 April 2020 | £160,000 | £355,000 |
  | 1 April 2022 | £170,000 | £375,000 |
  | 1 April 2023 | £190,000 | £415,000 |
  | 1 April 2024 | £195,000 | £430,000 |
  | 1 April 2025 | £200,000 | £445,000 |
  | 1 April 2026 | £205,000 | £455,000 |

  The table cites [[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]. Complaints referred before 2012 had a £100,000 cap, and those referred from 2012 to March 2019 a £150,000 cap.
- **Outside the cap.** Interest on the award, costs, and interest on costs ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
- **Above the cap.** The Ombudsman may recommend that the firm pays the balance, but cannot award it ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
- **Interest.** An award may carry interest from a set date, and interest on any amount paid late ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Interest awards).
- **Costs.** A costs award can cover costs the complainant reasonably incurred. Most complainants shouldn't need professional advisers, "so awards of costs are unlikely to be common" ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Costs awards).
- **Directions.** The Ombudsman can direct the firm to take steps it considers just and appropriate, "whether or not a court could order those steps to be taken" ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Directions).
- **Compliance.** A firm must comply promptly with any award or direction, and with any settlement it agrees earlier. A complainant can enforce through the courts a money award registered by the Ombudsman, or a direction ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Complying with awards and settlements).
