## Summary
- **What it is.** The Financial Services and Markets Act 2000 (2000 c. 8), "an Act to make provision about the regulation of financial services and markets", enacted on 14 June 2000 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=1]]). This file is the revised text, up to date with all changes known to be in force on or before 10 September 2026 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=1]]).
- **Prospective text.** The file says it "contains provisions that are prospective": text that may be brought into force at a future date ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=1]]). It ends with a list of changes not yet in the text, such as a Schedule 19D inserted by S.I. 2026/480, which the contents doesn't list ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=1263]]; [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-contents-2026-10.md]]).
- **How it is organised.** Thirty numbered Parts, I to XXX, with lettered Parts inserted between them (1A, 4A, 5A, 7A, 8A, 8B, 9A–9D, 12A, 12B, 13A, 15A, 16A, 17A, 18A, 20A, 20B). Then 36 Schedules, numbered 1 to 22 with lettered Schedules between them ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-contents-2026-10.md]]). Each section is followed by its annotations: textual amendments, modifications and commencement information ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=3]], [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=592]]).
- **Parts replaced by others.** Part I (ss. 1–18) is empty. Part 1A was substituted for it by the Financial Services Act 2012 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=3]], F1). Part IV (ss. 40–55) is also empty, with Part 4A substituted for it by the same Act ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=80]], F221).
