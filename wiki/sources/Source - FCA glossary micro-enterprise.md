**Raw file:** [[raw/fca-glossary-micro-enterprise.md]] · **Author or publisher:** Financial Conduct Authority, FCA Handbook Glossary ("micro-enterprise", G2623) · **Published:** undated; retrieved 2026-10-14 · **Trust:** primary (a regulator's own rules) · **Ingested:** 2026-10-14

## Summary
This is the FCA Handbook Glossary definition of "micro-enterprise". It is the smallest business tier that can complain to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and it comes from the Payment Services Directive ([[raw/fca-glossary-micro-enterprise.md]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Eligible complainant]].

## Key claims
- **Definition.** An enterprise that employs fewer than 10 persons **and** has a turnover or annual balance sheet that does not exceed €2 million ([[raw/fca-glossary-micro-enterprise.md]]).
- **How to apply it.** Articles 3 to 6 of the Annex to the Micro-enterprise Recommendation must be applied ([[raw/fca-glossary-micro-enterprise.md]]).
- **Origin.** Article 4(36) of the Payment Services Directive and the Annex to the Micro-enterprise Recommendation ([[raw/fca-glossary-micro-enterprise.md]]).
