# Source - FOS How to complain

**Raw file:** [[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]] · **Author or publisher:** Financial Ombudsman Service (financial-ombudsman.org.uk) · **Published:** undated; retrieved 2026-10-14 · **Trust:** primary (an organisation's own statement about itself). Your decision, D-094: where it restates the FCA's rules, DISP is the FCA's own statement and is proposed first · **Ingested:** 2026-10-14

## Summary
The [[wiki/entities/Financial Ombudsman Service]]'s own three-step guide for consumers: complain to the business, wait while it investigates, then bring the complaint to the FOS. It restates the firm's deadlines in plain words. Two of those restatements differ from DISP; see Conflicts below ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).

## Key claims
- **Step 1: the business first.** Contact the business, explain the problem and say how you want it put right. The business "should have the chance to sort things out", and most complaints are resolved directly with it. Keep records of calls, emails and events ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Step 2: wait.** Firms must follow rules when they investigate, consider complaints quickly and fairly, and keep you updated. If you tell the firm you are vulnerable, for example severely ill, in financial difficulty or in urgent need, it may be able to look at the complaint sooner ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **The final response.** It gives the outcome and how to take the complaint further. The FOS will ask to see it ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Six months.** "You will need to make a complaint to us within 6 months from the date on your final response" ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Firm deadlines, as the FOS puts them.** "For most complaints, a business has up to 8 weeks". It has "only 15 days" for complaints about fraud and scams, payment services and electronic money. If there is no response in time, you can refer the complaint ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]). Two parts of this differ from DISP, and DISP was stated over them on 2026-10-14; see Conflicts.
- **Step 3: bring it to the FOS.** You can do so after an unsatisfactory final response, or when the business has had its time and not replied ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Free, no representative needed.** The service is free and easy to use. You don't need to pay a lawyer or a claims management company, though a family member or friend can help ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Use of AI.** The FOS prefers complaints in the complainant's own words. AI tools "can sometimes get things wrong or add unnecessary legal sounding arguments", which can cause delay. If you use AI, use it only to organise and clarify, check the result carefully, and don't enter sensitive personal information. You are responsible for its accuracy ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Online form.** It checks you are ready, gives an idea of whether the FOS can help, and asks about the complaint, what happened and the business ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).

## Conflicts and open points
- **15 days or 15 business days (fact).** This page says "only 15 days" for payment-services and e-money complaints. DISP 1.6.2AR says 15 business days, or 35 in exceptional circumstances ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR). Resolved 2026-10-14, [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] conflict 1: DISP's 15 business days is stated, and this page's "15 days" is marked outweighed, as DISP is the FCA's own statement. See [[wiki/concepts/Complaint handling time limits]].
- **Fraud and scams on the 15-day clock (fact).** This page puts all fraud and scam complaints on the short clock. DISP applies it to EMD and PSD complaints ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR), and it recognises authorised push payment fraud complaints that are not PSD complaints ([[raw/fca-disp-2-7-complainant-eligibility.md]], DISP 2.7.6R(2B)). Resolved 2026-10-14, [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] conflict 2: DISP's scope (EMD and PSD complaints only) is stated, and this page's "fraud and scams" is marked outweighed, as DISP is the FCA's own statement. See [[wiki/concepts/Complaint handling time limits]].
- **Six months, worded differently.** The page counts six months "from the date on your final response" ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]). DISP counts from the date the firm sent it ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.2R(1)). Read together, these don't clash.
