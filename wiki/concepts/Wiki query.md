# Wiki query

The operation of asking a question against the wiki: the LLM finds relevant pages, reads them, and synthesizes a cited answer ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).

## What the sources say
- Answers can take different output forms depending on the question — a markdown page, a comparison table, a slide deck, a chart, a canvas ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).
- Good answers should be filed back into the wiki as new pages rather than left in chat history, so explorations compound the knowledge base the same way ingested sources do ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).
