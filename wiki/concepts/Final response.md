# Final response

A final response is a firm's written answer to a complaint. It accepts the complaint, offers redress, or rejects it with reasons, and it starts the complainant's six months to go to the Ombudsman ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1); [[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.2R(1)).

## What the sources say

### What it must contain (emphasis)
- **The decision.** One of the following ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1)(a)–(c)):
  - accept the complaint and, where appropriate, offer redress or remedial action;
  - offer redress without accepting the complaint;
  - reject the complaint and give reasons.
- **The FOS information** ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1)(d)–(f)):
  - the FOS's standard explanatory leaflet, enclosed;
  - the FOS website address;
  - a statement that the complainant may now refer the complaint to the FOS;
  - whether the firm consents to waive the DISP 2.8 time limits, in the DISP 1 Annex 3R wording.
- **The six-month limit.** It must tell the complainant about the six-month limit for going to the FOS ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]]).
- **Prominence.** The FOS information must be set out clearly, comprehensibly, accessibly and prominently in the text ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]).
- **Format for payment services and e-money.** The response must be on paper, or on another durable medium if agreed ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR).

### When it isn't ready
- **At eight weeks.** The firm must send a written response that explains why, says when it expects to answer, tells the complainant they may now go to the FOS, says whether it would waive the time limits, and encloses the leaflet and website address ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(2)).
- **For payment services and e-money.** In exceptional circumstances, a holding response within 15 business days gives the reasons for the delay and the deadline for the final response ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR(2)).

### What it does
- **For the complainant.** The FOS tells consumers that a final response gives the outcome and how to take the complaint further, to read it carefully and keep it, and that the FOS will ask to see it ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **It starts the clock.** Only a final response, a redress determination or a summary resolution communication starts the six-month referral limit ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]]).
- **It opens the door.** Once it is sent, the Ombudsman can consider the complaint, whatever the deadline ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R(1)).
