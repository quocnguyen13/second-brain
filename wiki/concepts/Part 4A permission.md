# Part 4A permission

The permission, given by the FCA or the PRA under Part 4A of FSMA, to carry on one or more regulated activities ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(1), (5)).

## What the sources say

### Getting one
- **Apply to the appropriate regulator.** The PRA when the activities "consist of or include a PRA-regulated activity", or the applicant is already PRA-authorised otherwise than by a Part 4A permission; the FCA in any other case ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(2)).
- **Who gives it.** The FCA gives permission on its own, consulting the PRA if the applicant's group includes a PRA-authorised person. The PRA gives it only "with the consent of the FCA" ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], ss. 55E(2)–(3), 55F(2)). See [[wiki/concepts/New bank authorisation]].
- **The test.** In giving or varying permission, imposing or varying a requirement, or giving consent, each regulator must ensure the firm will satisfy, and continue to satisfy, the [[wiki/concepts/Threshold Conditions]] it is responsible for. The duty doesn't stop it acting to advance its objectives ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=84]], s. 55B(3)–(4)).
- **What it says.** The permission specifies the permitted activities, described as the regulator thinks fit, possibly with limitations, more narrowly or widely than applied for, or with an activity not applied for ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=86]], s. 55E(4)–(5); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=87]], s. 55F(3)–(4)).
- **One per firm.** A firm with a Part 4A permission in force can't apply for another; it applies to vary the one it has ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(2); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I(1)).
- **It decides solo or dual.** A "PRA-authorised person" is one whose permission covers at least one PRA-regulated activity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2B(5)). See [[wiki/concepts/Solo and dual regulation]].

### Changing it at the firm's request (emphasis)
- **A new product outside the permission.** To carry on a regulated activity its permission doesn't cover, a firm applies to vary the permission by adding the activity or changing its description: to the FCA if it isn't PRA-authorised and the activity isn't PRA-regulated; to the PRA, which needs the FCA's consent, if the firm is PRA-authorised or the new activity is PRA-regulated ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(2); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(3)). Carrying it on without the variation breaches a regulator's requirement ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=52]], s. 20(1)). See [[wiki/concepts/General prohibition]].
- **Grounds to refuse.** The FCA may refuse to advance its operational objectives; the PRA to advance any of its objectives; and the FCA may withhold consent to a PRA variation to advance its operational objectives ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(4); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(4)–(5)).
- **Cancelling.** The firm may apply to cancel: to the FCA if it isn't PRA-authorised, or to the PRA, which consults the FCA ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=88]], s. 55H(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I(2)). A permission left with no activities must be cancelled once it is no longer needed ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55H(6); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55I(6)).

### Applying and the deadlines (emphasis)
- **What to file.** An application for permission states the regulated activities the applicant proposes to carry on and gives a UK address for service; one for a variation states the desired variation and the activities it would carry on afterwards ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=108]], s. 55U(1)–(2)). The regulator directs the manner and may require further information until it decides; for an application to the PRA, either regulator may ask ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=108]], s. 55U(4)–(6)).
- **The statutory clock.** The regulator must decide within 6 months of receiving the completed application. It may decide an incomplete one, and must decide it within 12 months of receipt. Where the other regulator's consent is needed, that consent decision falls within the same period ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(1)–(3)). Insurance-distribution-only applications have 3 months ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(9)). These are maximums; the Act gives no typical duration.
- **The outcome.** A grant comes by written notice stating its effective date; a PRA notice must say the FCA has consented ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(5)–(7)). A proposed refusal, or a grant narrower than asked or with requirements attached, comes first as a warning notice, then a decision notice ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=111]], s. 55X(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=112]], s. 55X(2), (4)). The applicant can withdraw before the decision ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=110]], s. 55V(4)).
- **Challenge.** An aggrieved applicant, or a firm aggrieved by an own-initiative variation or requirement, may refer the matter to the Tribunal ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=118]], s. 55Z3(1)–(2)).

### The own-initiative powers (emphasis)
- **Grounds.** Either regulator may act on a firm that is failing, or likely to fail, its threshold conditions; that hasn't carried on a permitted activity for at least 12 months; or where acting is desirable to advance its objectives (the FCA's operational objectives, any of the PRA's) ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=90]], s. 55J(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L(2); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=98]], s. 55M(2)).
- **Own-initiative variation power (s. 55J).** To add, remove or re-describe activities, or cancel the permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(2)–(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=94]], s. 55J(12)). Each regulator consults the other where the firm is dual-regulated (the FCA also for a member of its group), and needs the other's consent only to add an activity or widen a description ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(4)–(5)). Further sector grounds apply to investment firms, AIFMs, mortgage intermediaries, UCITS managers and benchmark administrators ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(6)–(6A); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=92]], s. 55J(6B); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=93]], s. 55J(7ZA)–(7ZC)). Schedule 6A gives the FCA an additional power over FCA-only firms ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=96]], s. 55JA).
- **Own-initiative requirement power (ss. 55L–55M).** To impose, vary or cancel requirements: the FCA on any firm with a Part 4A permission, the PRA on a PRA-authorised person ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L(2)–(4); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=98]], s. 55M(2)–(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=99]], s. 55M(4)).
- **What a requirement can do.** Require specified action or restraint, reach unregulated activities, be set by reference to the group, expire after a period, and refer to past conduct, such as a review of, or remedial action for, past business ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=99]], s. 55N(1)–(5)). Requirements can also be imposed when a permission is given or varied ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=97]], s. 55L(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=98]], s. 55M(1)).
- **Procedure.** The firm gets written notice with reasons, the effective date, and its rights to make representations and refer to the Tribunal. Action takes immediate effect only if the regulator reasonably considers that necessary ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=114]], s. 55Y(2)–(3); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=115]], s. 55Y(4)–(5)). Cancelling other than at the firm's request needs a warning notice and a decision notice ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=116]], s. 55Z(1)–(2)).
- **Further reach.** After a change of control with an uncertain effect, the regulator may impose requirements it could have imposed on grant ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=102]], s. 55O(1)–(2)). A requirement may freeze the firm's assets or put them with a trustee ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=103]], s. 55P(4)). The powers may be used for an overseas regulator ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=105]], s. 55Q(1)), and a regulator may weigh persons connected with the firm ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=106]], s. 55R(1)).
- **The PRA's account.** It may vary a firm's permission or impose a requirement under Part 4A to prevent or curtail certain regulated activities, which may require a change to the firm's business model or strategy; it may refuse requested changes that could adversely affect the firm's safety and soundness ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=50]], paras 143–144). It will use formal powers early where appropriate, not only as a last resort ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=50]], para 145; [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=51]], para 145).

### Coordination
- **In the MoU.** The FCA–PRA memorandum may cover applications for Part 4A permission, variation of permission and requirements ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3E(2)). It does: for dual-regulated firms the PRA leads a single process and communicates all notices and decisions ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=21]], para 1). See [[wiki/concepts/FCA-PRA coordination]].
- **A separate permission for promotions.** Permission to approve other people's financial promotions is given by the FCA under s. 55NA and is not a Part 4A permission ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=83]], s. 55A(6); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=100]], s. 55NA(1)). See [[wiki/concepts/Financial promotion]].
