# Ombudsman awards

An Ombudsman award is what the Financial Ombudsman Service can order when it upholds a complaint: a money award up to a cap, interest, costs, or a direction to the firm ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).

## What the sources say
- **Money.** Fair compensation for financial loss, pain and suffering, damage to reputation, and distress or inconvenience, "whether or not a court would award compensation" ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Money awards).
- **The cap.** For complaints referred on or after 1 April 2026: £455,000 for acts on or after 1 April 2019, and £205,000 for earlier acts. The cap rises with CPI each 1 April, rounded down to £5,000 ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
- **Outside the cap.** Interest and costs don't count towards it. Above it, the Ombudsman can only recommend that the firm pays the balance ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
- **Directions.** The firm can be told to take steps the Ombudsman considers just and appropriate, even ones a court couldn't order ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]], Directions).
- **Costs.** These are rare, because complainants "should not need to have professional advisers" ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
- **Binding on the firm.** It must comply promptly, including with settlements it agreed earlier, and the complainant can enforce a registered money award or a direction in the courts ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]).
