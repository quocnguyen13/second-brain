# Complaint handling time limits

These are the deadlines a firm has to answer a complaint: eight weeks for most complaints, and 15 business days for payment-services and e-money complaints, or 35 in exceptional circumstances ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R, 1.6.2AR).

## What the sources say
- **Acknowledge, then inform.** On receipt, a firm must send a prompt written acknowledgement that states when it will respond, and then keep the complainant informed ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.1R).
- **Most complaints: eight weeks.** By the end of eight weeks, the firm must send either a [[wiki/concepts/Final response]] or a written response explaining the delay ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R). Almost all complaints are expected to be substantively addressed within that time ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]). The FOS gives consumers the same figure: "up to 8 weeks" ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Payment services and e-money: 15 business days.** A final response must be sent by the end of 15 business days after the day the firm received the complaint. If reasons beyond the firm's control prevent that, it must send a holding response within 15 business days, giving the reasons and a deadline, and then a final response within 35 business days. The final response must be on paper or another agreed durable medium ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR).
- **Which complaints.** The short deadline applies where a complaint is an EMD complaint or a PSD complaint, and any other complaint has eight weeks ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.1R, 1.6.2AR). DISP recognises complaints about an alleged authorised push payment fraud that are not PSD complaints ([[raw/fca-disp-2-7-complainant-eligibility.md]], DISP 2.7.6R(2B)). The FOS's consumer wording ("15 days", including "fraud and scams") was set aside; see "Where sources disagree".
- **Mixed complaints.** The payment-services part must follow the shorter rule. The firm may put the whole complaint on the shorter clock, and must tell the complainant if it deals with it in parts ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]). If it does, the Ombudsman's timing rules treat the whole complaint as a payment-services complaint ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]]).
- **What a missed deadline means.** Once the deadline has passed, the complainant can go to the Ombudsman without a final response ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R; [[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Ombudsman referral time limits]].
- **Vulnerable customers.** The FOS tells consumers that a firm may look at a complaint sooner if it knows they are vulnerable, for example severely ill, in financial difficulty or in urgent need ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
- **Supervision.** The FCA may judge a firm's complaint handling on quality as well as speed ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]]).

## Where sources disagree
- **1. 15 days or 15 business days (fact).**
  - A: a final response "by the end of 15 business days after the day on which it received the complaint", or 35 business days in exceptional circumstances, with a holding response ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR) · primary · last updated 2026-06-01
  - B: a business has "only 15 days" to consider complaints about payment services and electronic money ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]) · primary · retrieved 2026-10-14 · outweighed by ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR), its own statement
  - Resolved 2026-10-14, [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] conflict 1.
- **2. Which complaints get the short deadline (fact).**
  - A: the short deadline applies where a complaint "is an EMD complaint or a PSD complaint"; any other complaint has eight weeks ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.1R, 1.6.2AR). DISP separately recognises complaints about an alleged authorised push payment fraud that are not PSD complaints ([[raw/fca-disp-2-7-complainant-eligibility.md]], DISP 2.7.6R(2B)) · primary · last updated 2026-06-01 and 2025-11-28
  - B: a business has "only 15 days" to consider complaints about "fraud and scams", as well as payment services and e-money ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]) · primary · retrieved 2026-10-14 · outweighed by ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2AR), its own statement
  - Resolved 2026-10-14, [[system/ingest/set-2026-10-14]] conflict 2.
