# Solo and dual regulation

Under FSMA, every authorised person is supervised by the FCA. Those whose permission includes at least one PRA-regulated activity are also supervised by the PRA ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 1L(1), 2B(5), 2K). "Solo-regulated" and "dual-regulated" are the two cases; the Act doesn't use those words, but the FCA–PRA memorandum does ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=6]]).

## What the sources say
- **The criterion.** A "PRA-authorised person" is an authorised person with permission, under Part 4A or any other provision of the Act, to carry on regulated activities that "consist of or include one or more PRA-regulated activities (see section 22A)" ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2B(5)). One PRA-regulated activity is enough. The test is on the firm's permission, not on each product.
- **The FCA's reach.** The FCA must supervise authorised persons, with no carve-out for PRA-authorised persons ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 1L(1)).
- **The PRA's reach.** The PRA must supervise PRA-authorised persons ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2K).
- **What follows:**
  - authorised with no PRA-regulated activity: FCA only (solo)
  - authorised with one or more PRA-regulated activities: FCA and PRA (dual)

  ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 1L(1), 2B(5), 2K)
- **Moving the line.** The Treasury can make further activities PRA-regulated by order under s. 22A ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2D(1)). For a dual-regulated firm, it can specify which matters are, or are mainly, one regulator's responsibility ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3G).
- **Insurance is contingent.** The PRA's insurance objective and the with-profits provisions apply "only if" effecting or carrying out insurance contracts is a PRA-regulated activity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 2C(3), 3F(4)).
- **What dual regulation means in practice.** It brings in the coordination machinery in [[wiki/concepts/FCA-PRA coordination]]:
  - a joint memorandum of understanding
  - the PRA's power to stop FCA action against PRA-authorised persons
  - directions on consolidated supervision of groups

  ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 3E, 3I, 3M)
- **Which firms the PRA regulates.** Around 1,292 banks, building societies, credit unions, insurers and major investment firms, by the PRA's own count as of 2026-09-21 ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]). Because the FCA supervises every authorised person, those firms answer to both regulators ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 1L(1), 2K).
- **The PRA's own description of its population.** It is the prudential regulator for deposit-takers, insurance companies and designated investment firms ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=7]]): large UK banking groups with ring-fenced bodies, overseas firms from global investment banks to small retail operations, building societies, and many hundreds of credit unions ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=10]]).
- **For a dual-regulated bank.** The FCA is its conduct regulator; the PRA its prudential regulator. Each focuses on the risks to its own objectives ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=16]]). Authorisation, and approval of the senior managers the PRA approves, need both regulators satisfied ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=23]]).
- **The regulators' own definitions.** Dual-regulated firms are "authorised by the PRA and regulated by both the PRA and the FCA"; solo-regulated firms are "authorised and regulated by the FCA only". The PRA is lead regulator for the first, the FCA for the second ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=6]]). Who leads on each process is tabled in [[wiki/concepts/FCA-PRA coordination]].
- **Firm types on each side.** The PRA authorises (with the FCA) and prudentially supervises deposit-takers (banks, building societies, credit unions), insurers (including friendly societies) and designated investment firms ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=4]]). The FCA prudentially supervises firms that are not PRA-regulated ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=3]]).
- **Groups can mix both.** In some groups, some entities are prudentially supervised by the PRA and others by the FCA, with the PRA as consolidated supervisor ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=6]]).
- **How many firms in each group.** The FCA regulates the conduct of about 35,500 firms and prudentially supervises about 34,000 ([[raw/fca-about-the-fca.md]]). The page doesn't say that the gap between the two figures is the PRA's population.
- **Which activities are PRA-regulated.** The sources now name the firm types (deposit-takers, insurers, designated investment firms: [[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=4]]) and treat "deposit-taking, insurance, certain large-scale investment business" as the PRA-regulated activities ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=15]]). Still general knowledge, not in any source: payment institutions, e-money institutions and consumer credit firms that take no deposits are FCA-only. Section 22A and the PRA-Regulated Activities Order would confirm this.
