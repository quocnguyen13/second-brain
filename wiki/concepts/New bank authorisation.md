# New bank authorisation

How a firm gets permission to take deposits in the UK: one application, assessed jointly by the PRA (prudential) and the FCA (conduct), led and decided by the PRA ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).

## What the sources say
- **Apply to the PRA.** Firms wishing to take deposits must apply to the PRA for authorisation ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).
- **Joint assessment.** The PRA assesses from a prudential perspective; the FCA focuses on conduct. The firm is authorised only if both are satisfied it will meet their [[wiki/concepts/Threshold Conditions]] at authorisation and on an ongoing basis ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).
- **Resolvable from day one.** The assessment includes whether the firm could be resolved in an orderly way ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Resolvability]].
- **PRA leads.** As provided for in the MoU, the PRA leads a single administrative process and is the decision maker ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).
- **The MoU's steps.** For a dual-regulated firm the PRA leads a single administrative process and communicates all notices and decisions. It must obtain the FCA's consent before granting a permission, and consent means the FCA can veto: disagreements must be resolved before the decision. Each regulator assesses the application against its own Threshold Conditions; the PRA usually asks for any extra information, and each shares what is material to the other ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=21]]).
- **Early engagement.** The PRA and FCA offer pre-application meetings to help produce a complete application; the PRA answers questions but gives no consultancy ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).
- **Proportionate.** All applicants get a minimum assessment; beyond that, work scales with the potential impact of the firm's failure. The aim is to keep barriers to entry to the minimum consistent with the PRA's objectives ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).
- **New Bank Start-up Unit.** A joint initiative of the regulators to help new banks enter the market and through their early days ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=42]]).
- **Senior managers.** People in SMF roles that the PRA approves need both regulators satisfied ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=23]]); the FCA alone approves customer-facing SMFs ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=22]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Senior Managers and Certification Regime]].
- **Overseas banks.** Branches are generally not allowed to take retail and small-company deposits beyond de minimis levels; material deposit-taking needs a subsidiary ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=56]]).
