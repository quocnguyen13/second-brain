# Ombudsman referral time limits

These are the rules on when a complaint can go to the [[wiki/entities/Financial Ombudsman Service]]: not before the firm has had its chance, and not after six months from its final response, or six years from the event or three years from awareness ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R, 2.8.2R).

## What the sources say
- **Too early.** The Ombudsman can consider a complaint only after one of these ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R):
  - the firm's final response or summary resolution communication;
  - eight weeks, for most complaints;
  - 15 business days with no holding response, or 35 business days after a holding response, for payment-services and e-money complaints.

  It can take a complaint earlier only if the firm consents and the complainant, told that the firm may be quicker, still wants the Ombudsman to deal with it. The firm's own deadlines are in [[wiki/concepts/Complaint handling time limits]].
- **Too late.** The Ombudsman cannot consider a complaint referred to it ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.2R):
  - more than six months after the firm's final response. The FOS tells consumers this as "within 6 months from the date on your final response" ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]);
  - more than six years after the event, or, if later, three years after the complainant knew or ought to have known. This limit doesn't apply if the complainant complained within it and has a record of the complaint.
- **Ways round the limits** ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.2R(3)–(5)):
  - exceptional circumstances, such as incapacity;
  - the Ombudsman Transitional Order;
  - the firm's consent, which cannot be withdrawn and is not available for a "relevant complaint" under s. 404B(3).
- **The firm controls the six-month start.** Only a final response, redress determination or summary resolution communication starts the six months, and the response must tell the complainant about the limit ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]]). A [[wiki/concepts/Final response]] also has to say whether the firm consents to waive the limits ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1)(f)).
- **Special regimes.** The pensions review and FSAVC review are outside the six-year and three-year limits. Mortgage endowment "high risk" letters can start a three-year clock. PPI sales made on or before 29 August 2017 had a 29 August 2019 deadline, with exceptions ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]]).
