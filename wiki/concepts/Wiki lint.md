# Wiki lint

The operation of periodically health-checking the wiki so it stays consistent as it grows ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).

## What the sources say
- Checks for: contradictions between pages, stale claims that newer sources have superseded, orphan pages with no inbound links, important concepts mentioned but lacking their own page, missing cross-references, and data gaps that could be filled with a new source or web search ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).
- The LLM is described as good at suggesting new questions to investigate and new sources to look for as part of this check ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).
