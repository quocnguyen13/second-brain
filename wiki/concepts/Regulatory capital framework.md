# Regulatory capital framework

The UK framework of minimum capital and buffers that the PRA sets for banks, expressed against risk-weighted assets and complemented by a leverage ratio ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=30]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=32]]).

## What the sources say
- **Four parts** ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=30]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=31]]):
  - **Pillar 1:** credit, market and operational risk, calculated by internationally agreed methods
  - **Pillar 2A:** PRA-set requirements for risks Pillar 1 misses, such as interest rate risk in the banking book or pension risk
  - **Combined buffer:** capital conservation and countercyclical buffers, plus G-SII or O-SII buffers where relevant
  - **PRA buffer:** firm-specific, can include a scalar for significantly weak risk management or governance
- **Minimum.** Pillar 1 plus Pillar 2A is the minimum to hold at all times ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=31]]).
- **Using buffers.** The PRA buffer is not public and using it triggers no automatic restriction. Drawing on the public combined buffer restricts dividends, AT1 coupons and discretionary bonuses. A firm below its buffers should prepare a capital restoration plan ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=31]]).
- **Quality.** Most capital should be ordinary shares and reserves ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=28]]).
- **Firm responsibility first.** Firms should judge their own capital adequacy and not rely on regulatory minima ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=29]]).
- **Internal models.** The PRA is "generally sceptical" that internal models alone are an appropriate basis for capital, and expects them to be conservative ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=32]]).
- **Smaller firms.** Supervisors may reduce Pillar 2A for firms on the standardised approach; 42 firms had a reduction as of February 2022 ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=31]]).
- **Leverage.** The UK minimum leverage ratio is 3.25%, excluding qualifying central bank claims; firms outside the binding requirement should not ordinarily fall below 3.25%, with at least 75% met in CET1 ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=33]]).
