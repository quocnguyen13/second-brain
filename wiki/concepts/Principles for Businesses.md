# Principles for Businesses

The FCA's "general statement of the fundamental obligations of firms under the regulatory system", set out in its Handbook and deriving their authority from its rule-making powers under FSMA ([[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=103]], Annex A 2.2).

## What the sources say
- **Principle 2.** A firm must conduct its business with due skill, care and diligence ([[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=103]], Annex A 2.2(a)).
- **Principle 3.** A firm must take reasonable care to organise and control its affairs responsibly and effectively, with adequate risk management systems ([[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=104]], Annex A 2.2(b)).
- **Breach brings a penalty.** A firm in breach of a Principle has contravened a requirement under the Act, which lets the FCA impose a penalty under s. 206 ([[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=1]], para 1.1; [[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=103]], Annex A 1.2).
- **Applied: TSB, 2022.** The FCA found that [[wiki/entities/TSB Bank]] breached Principle 2 in managing its outsourcer and in a testing decision, and Principle 3 in planning, governance, risk management and business continuity for its 2018 migration ([[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=10]], [[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=11]], paras 2.33–2.36). It judged the breaches negligent, not deliberate or reckless ([[raw/fca-final-notice-tsb-2022.pdf#page=99]], paras 6.13–6.14).
- **Principle 11.** It requires firms to tell the FCA anything it would reasonably expect to know ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).

The text of the other Principles is not in `raw/`.
