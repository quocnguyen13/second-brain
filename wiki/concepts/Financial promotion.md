# Financial promotion

An invitation or inducement, communicated in the course of business, to engage in investment activity or claims management activity. FSMA s. 21 restricts it to authorised persons, or to content an authorised person has approved ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=53]], s. 21(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(2)).

## What the sources say
- **The restriction.** A person must not, in the course of business, communicate an invitation or inducement to engage in investment activity or in claims management activity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=53]], s. 21(1); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(1)(b)). The claims management limb extends to England, Wales and Scotland ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=55]], F135).
- **Who may communicate.**
  - an authorised person, in its own right
  - anyone, if an authorised person has approved the content ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(2))
- **The approver gateway.** An authorised person may approve another's promotion only if the approval is permitted under s. 55NA, with FCA permission, or falls within an exemption in regulations under s. 55NB ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(2A)). Inserted by the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023; fully in force since 7 February 2024 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=55]], F136).
- **Reach.** "Communicate" includes causing a communication to be made ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=55]], s. 21(13)). A communication from outside the UK is caught only if it is capable of having an effect in the UK ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(3)).
- **Scope set by the Treasury.** "Engaging in investment activity" means entering or offering to enter an agreement that is a controlled activity, or exercising rights under a controlled investment; both are of kinds the Treasury specifies by order ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(8)–(10); [[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=55]], s. 21(15)). The Treasury may also specify by order when the restriction doesn't apply, which may include compliance with financial promotion rules ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=54]], s. 21(5)–(6)).
- **Cryptoassets.** "Investment" includes a cryptoasset ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=55]], s. 21(14)). Section 21 is modified for specified purposes from 25 February 2026 by the FSMA 2000 (Cryptoassets) Regulations 2026 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=55]], C78).
- **Breaching it is an offence.** Up to six months or a fine on summary conviction, up to two years or a fine on indictment ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=62]], s. 25(1)). Defences: a reasonable belief that the content was prepared, or approved in line with s. 21(2A), by an authorised person; or all reasonable precautions and due diligence ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=62]], s. 25(2)).
- **Deals that follow are unenforceable against the customer.** A customer who enters a controlled agreement, or exercises rights under a controlled investment, as a result of an unlawful promotion is not bound and can recover money paid plus compensation. The court may allow enforcement if just and equitable, weighing whether the communicator reasonably believed the promotion lawful, or whether the person enforcing knew of it ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=69]], s. 30(2)–(7)).
- **In the FCA's remit.** When an authorised person communicates, or approves others' communication of, invitations or inducements to engage in investment activity or claims management activity, that is a regulated financial service ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 1H(2)(c)). See [[wiki/concepts/Regulated financial services]].
