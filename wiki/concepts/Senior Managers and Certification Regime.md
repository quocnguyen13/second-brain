# Senior Managers and Certification Regime

The SM&CR ties seniority to accountability: firms allocate clear responsibilities to individuals in Senior Management Functions (SMFs), who need regulatory approval and carry a duty of responsibility ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=23]]).

## What the sources say
- **Duty of responsibility.** SMFs must take reasonable steps to prevent or stop breaches in their area, and delegate only to appropriate people they oversee ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=23]]).
- **Approval by both regulators.** Individuals must be approved before taking up an SMF role. Approval is granted only if both the PRA, as prudential regulator, and the FCA, as conduct regulator, are satisfied the person is fit and proper. Firms must check fitness and propriety first and on an ongoing basis ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=23]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=24]]).
- **Who leads, per the MoU.** For dual-regulated firms, lead responsibility splits by which regulator's rules designate the SMF ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=6]]). The PRA designates SMFs where the role involves a risk of serious consequences (s. 59ZA), leads their approval and obtains the FCA's consent. The FCA designates SMFs tied to the customer interface, including client assets, anti-money laundering and compliance, approves them alone, and consults the PRA for a subset ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=21]], [[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=22]]). Each consults the other before withdrawing an approval the other gave ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=26]]).
- **Management Responsibilities Map.** Firms must keep one showing each senior individual's responsibilities and reporting lines ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=24]]).
- **Branches.** Overseas banks' UK branches should appoint an SMF 19, Head of an overseas branch ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=25]]).
- **Discipline.** The PRA can fine, censure, withdraw approval and prohibit SMFs who breach its Conduct Rules ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=25]]).
- **In supervision.** After each Periodic Summary Meeting the firm must allocate an SMF to each key risk, reflected in that SMF's Statement of Responsibilities ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=48]]). Recovery planning is a prescribed responsibility ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=38]]).
