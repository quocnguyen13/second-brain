# Prudential Regulation Committee

The Bank of England committee through which the Bank acts as the PRA, and which takes the PRA's most important decisions ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2A(2); [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=48]]).

## What the sources say
- **Legal basis.** The PRA's functions are exercisable only through the PRC under Part 3A of the Bank of England Act 1998 ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2A(2)).
- **What it decides.** It sets the PRA levy by rules, adopts the PRA budget with the approval of Court, makes key supervisory decisions on the largest firms, and holds non-delegable responsibilities including high-level strategy and policy-making ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=48]]).
- **Accountability.** It is accountable to Parliament, like the MPC and FPC, and must report annually to the Chancellor on how independent the PRA's functions are from the Bank's other functions ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=48]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=15]]).
- **Members.** Chaired by the Governor; with the Deputy Governors for Financial Stability, Markets Banking and Resolution, and Prudential Regulation; the FCA's Chief Executive; one member appointed by the Governor with the Chancellor's approval; and at least six members appointed by the Chancellor ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=48]]).
- **Decides the veto.** A PRA direction to the FCA under s. 3I is decided by the PRC ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=17]]). It is the PRA's statutory rule-making committee, and serious FCA–PRA policy conflicts can be escalated to it and the FCA Board ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=4]]). The FCA CEO sits on it ex officio ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=18]]).
- **Reviews firm assessments.** Depending on a firm's potential impact category, its periodic assessment may be reviewed by the PRC ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=47]]).
