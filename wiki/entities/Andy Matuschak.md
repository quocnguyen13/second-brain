# Andy Matuschak

Author of the "Evergreen notes" page on notes.andymatuschak.org. The attribution comes from the clip's URL; its author field is empty ([[raw/matuschak-evergreen-notes.md]]).

## What the sources say
- Defines [[wiki/concepts/Evergreen notes]] as notes written to evolve, contribute and accumulate over time, across projects ([[raw/matuschak-evergreen-notes.md]]).
- Frames the practice as developing insight rather than taking notes: what matters is "better thinking" ([[raw/matuschak-evergreen-notes.md]]).
- Credits the Zettelkasten as the concept's main debt ([[raw/matuschak-evergreen-notes.md]]).
