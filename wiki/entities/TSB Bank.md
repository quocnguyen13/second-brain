# TSB Bank

> [!warning] AI only: no source in raw/ backs this page yet.

A UK bank whose 2018 IT migration failed, leading to fines from both the FCA and the PRA for the same incident ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).

## What the sources say

### History
- TSB was split off from Lloyds Banking Group in 2014 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Sabadell bought TSB in 2015 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).

### The 2018 migration
- Over the weekend of 20–22 April 2018, TSB moved most of its operations and customer data from the Lloyds platform to a new one ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- The new platform was built by a Sabadell IT subsidiary ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- The data moved successfully, but the platform failed at once ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Branch, telephone, online and mobile banking were disrupted ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- All branches were affected, and so was a large share of TSB's 5.2 million customers ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Normal service returned only in December 2018 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).

### Two regulators, one incident
- In December 2022 the FCA fined TSB £29.75m ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- In December 2022 the PRA fined TSB £18.9m ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- The total of £48.65m was after a 30% settlement discount ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- **The FCA's framing: conduct.**
  - The FCA found a breach of Principle 2 (due skill, care and diligence), mainly in overseeing the outsourcer and in a testing decision ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - It found a breach of Principle 3 (organising and controlling the programme with adequate risk management) ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - TSB received 225,492 complaints within a year ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - It paid about £32.7m in redress ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - Its plans for identifying vulnerable customers during a long incident were weak ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- **The PRA's framing: prudential.**
  - The PRA treated the same events as a safety-and-soundness failure ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - The Bank's release said governance, operational risk, business continuity and outsourcing management have long been part of prudential rules ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - The PRA's general expectation that firms manage operational as well as financial risks is in `raw/`: see [[wiki/concepts/Operational resilience]]. That the release said so about TSB is not.

### What drove the decisions (from the FCA notice, per the note)
- The migration plan was built backwards from a target date ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- TSB publicly committed to a new migration window before its re-plan was finished ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- A cut to one type of performance testing was treated as purely technical and decided outside the proper governance forum ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- That testing would probably have caught the configuration problem that later took digital banking down ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- To keep its internal-ratings-based capital approach, TSB had to migrate by June 2018 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- A successful exit was expected to release extra capital the PRA had required because TSB relied on another bank's platform ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
