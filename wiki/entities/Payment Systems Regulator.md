# Payment Systems Regulator

The UK regulator of payment systems, set up by the FCA as a separate entity in April 2015 ([[raw/wikipedia-financial-conduct-authority.md]] · commentary).

## What the sources say
- **Origin.** Created by the FCA in April 2015 under s. 40 of the Financial Services (Banking Reform) Act 2013 ([[raw/wikipedia-financial-conduct-authority.md]] · commentary).
- **Role.** "To promote competition and innovation in payment systems, and ensure they work in the interests of the organisations and people that use them" ([[raw/wikipedia-financial-conduct-authority.md]] · commentary).
- **Access powers.** A 2015 amendment removed a restriction so its access powers reach any payment system covered by the access provisions of the 2007 Payment Services Directive ([[raw/wikipedia-financial-conduct-authority.md]] · commentary).
- **APP fraud.** From May 2019 some victims of authorised push payment fraud could get refunds under the voluntary Contingent Reimbursement Model, overseen by the PSR ([[raw/wikipedia-financial-conduct-authority.md]] · commentary).
- **Shared chair.** Charles Randell chaired both the FCA and the PSR from April 2018 ([[raw/wikipedia-financial-conduct-authority.md]] · commentary).
- **In FSMA.** Public consultations by the FCA must disclose engagement with the PSR's statutory panels ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 1RB). People paid by the PSR can't sit on the regulators' statutory panels unless Treasury regulations allow ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 1MA, 2LA).
- **Its future (AI only).**
  - The [[wiki/entities/Financial Services and Markets Bill 2026–27]] would move the PSR into the FCA ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - The Bill was before the Commons as of 15 September 2026 and is not yet law ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
  - The PSR stays in place until the Act is passed and commenced ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
