# Anthropic

Publisher of the "Contextual Retrieval" engineering post and maker of the Claude models and API it describes ([[raw/anthropic-contextual-retrieval.md]]).

## What the sources say
- Proposed [[wiki/concepts/Contextual Retrieval]] and benchmarked it across codebases, fiction, ArXiv papers and science papers ([[raw/anthropic-contextual-retrieval.md]]).
- Released [[wiki/concepts/Prompt caching]] for Claude, which it says makes both long prompts and Contextual Retrieval cheaper ([[raw/anthropic-contextual-retrieval.md]]).
- Publishes a cookbook for implementing Contextual Retrieval with Claude ([[raw/anthropic-contextual-retrieval.md]]).
- The post credits Daniel Ford for research and writing ([[raw/anthropic-contextual-retrieval.md]]).
