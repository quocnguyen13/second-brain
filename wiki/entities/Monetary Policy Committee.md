# Monetary Policy Committee

The Bank of England committee through which the Bank mainly pursues monetary policy ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=1]]).

## What the sources say
- **Its role in the Bank.** The Bank's responsibilities for monetary policy and financial stability are pursued mainly through the Monetary Policy Committee and the [[wiki/entities/Financial Policy Committee]] ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=1]]).
- **Accountability.** It is accountable to Parliament, as are the FPC and the PRC ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=48]]).
- **Bank Rate.** It sets Bank Rate ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- **Target.** It sets Bank Rate to meet the government's 2% inflation target ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
