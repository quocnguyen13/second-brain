# Tiago Forte

Author of the PARA method post on the Forte Labs blog ([[raw/forte-para-method.md]]).

## What the sources say
- He says he developed [[wiki/concepts/PARA method]] over "more than a decade" of personal experimentation, teaching "thousands of students" and coaching professionals ([[raw/forte-para-method.md]]).
- He worked as a productivity coach in the San Francisco Bay Area and coached executives at a biotech firm ([[raw/forte-para-method.md]]).
- His first question to a coaching client is to see their project list ([[raw/forte-para-method.md]]).
