# Financial Policy Committee

The Bank of England committee with statutory responsibility for reducing risks to the financial system as a whole: macroprudential policy ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=15]]).

## What the sources say
- **Powers over the PRA.** It can make recommendations to the PRA, which may be on a "comply or explain" basis, and give directions on macroprudential tools specified by the Treasury in secondary legislation ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=15]]).
- **Two-way flow.** The PRA gives it firm-specific information; its view of systemic risk informs PRA judgements ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=15]]).
- **Leverage ratio.** The UK leverage ratio requirement is specified through an FPC direction and recommendation, which the FPC reviews annually ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=33]]).
- **Rule reviews.** Rules made to comply with an FPC direction or recommendation are outside the regulators' general duty to review rules ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3RA(2)).
- **Shared membership.** Common membership of the PRA and FCA CEOs on the FPC assists co-ordination ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=16]]).
