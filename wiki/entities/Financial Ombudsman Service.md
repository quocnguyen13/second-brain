# Financial Ombudsman Service

The Financial Ombudsman Service is the body that considers complaints against financial firms once the firm has had its chance to resolve them, within set time limits ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R, 2.8.2R).

## What the sources say
- **When it can act.** Only after the firm's final response, or after the firm's deadline has passed: eight weeks for most complaints, and 15 or 35 business days for payment-services and e-money complaints. It can act earlier only with the firm's consent ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.1R). See [[wiki/concepts/Ombudsman referral time limits]].
- **How complainants hear of it.** Every final response must enclose the FOS's standard explanatory leaflet, give its website address and tell the complainant they may now refer the complaint to it ([[raw/fca-disp-1-6-complaints-time-limits.md]], DISP 1.6.2R(1)). See [[wiki/concepts/Final response]].
- **When it can't.** It cannot take complaints referred more than six months after the final response. Nor can it take complaints referred more than six years after the event, or, if later, three years after awareness. The exceptions are exceptional circumstances or the firm's consent ([[raw/fca-disp-2-8-referral-time-limits.md]], DISP 2.8.2R).
- **Who can use it.** It deals only with complaints by or for an eligible complainant: consumers, micro-enterprises, small businesses, small charities and trusts, CBTL consumers and guarantors, whose complaint arises from a listed relationship with the firm ([[raw/fca-disp-2-7-complainant-eligibility.md]]). The Ombudsman decides a business's eligibility on evidence such as audited accounts or VAT returns ([[raw/fca-disp-2-7-complainant-eligibility.md]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Eligible complainant]].
- **What it can award.** Money up to a cap, which is £455,000 for acts on or after 1 April 2019 on complaints referred from 1 April 2026; interest; costs; and directions to the firm. Firms must comply promptly, and awards can be enforced in the courts ([[raw/fca-disp-3-7-ombudsman-awards.md]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Ombudsman awards]].
- **In its own words.** It asks consumers to complain to the business first, because most complaints are resolved there. Its service is free, and consumers don't need a lawyer or a claims management company. It prefers complaints in the complainant's own words, and warns that AI tools can add inaccurate "legal sounding arguments" that cause delay. Complaints are made through an online form ([[raw/financial-ombudsman-how-to-complain.md]]).
