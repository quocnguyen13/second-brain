# Vannevar Bush

Director of the U.S. Office of Scientific Research and Development, who in 1945 proposed the memex and argued for associative indexing over conventional filing ([[raw/bush-as-we-may-think.pdf]]).

## What the sources say
- As Director of OSRD, coordinated roughly six thousand American scientists applying science to WWII warfare, and wrote this piece urging scientists' postwar work toward making the "bewildering store of knowledge" accessible ([[raw/bush-as-we-may-think.pdf#page=1]]).
- Proposed the [[wiki/concepts/Memex]], a hypothetical desk-based device for storing and associatively linking a lifetime's books, records, and correspondence on microfilm ([[raw/bush-as-we-may-think.pdf#page=15]]).
- Argued indexing should mimic the mind's associative operation rather than alphabetic or numeric classification — see [[wiki/concepts/Associative indexing]] ([[raw/bush-as-we-may-think.pdf#page=14]]).
- [[wiki/sources/Source - LLM Wiki]] names his [[wiki/concepts/Memex]] as the forerunner of the compiled-wiki pattern it proposes ([[raw/karpathy-llm-wiki.md]]).
