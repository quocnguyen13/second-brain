# Bank of England

Acting through its Prudential Regulation Committee, the Bank of England is the Prudential Regulation Authority. In its other capacity it pursues a Financial Stability Objective ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 2A, 3Q).

## What the sources say
- **It is the PRA.** The "Prudential Regulation Authority" is the Bank of England. Its PRA functions are exercisable only through the Prudential Regulation Committee ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2A(1)–(2)).
- **Two capacities kept apart.** In FSMA, "the Bank of England" normally excludes the Bank acting as the PRA, and "the PRA" excludes the Bank acting in any other capacity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2A(3)–(4)).
- **Strategy.** The PRA must consult the Bank's court of directors on its strategy ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2E(2)).
- **Co-operation with the FCA.** The FCA must co-operate with the Bank on the Bank's Financial Stability Objective, which can include sharing information ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3Q). The FCA and the Bank may provide services to each other ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3R(2)).
- **Financial Policy Committee.** Rules made to comply with a direction or recommendation of the Bank's [[wiki/entities/Financial Policy Committee]] are outside the regulators' general duty to review rules ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3RA(2)). The FPC can make recommendations and give directions to the PRA ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=15]]).
- **Through the PRA.** The Bank describes itself as prudentially regulating and supervising financial services firms through the PRA ([[raw/bank-of-england-prudential-regulation.md]]).
- **Its responsibilities, per the MoU.** Monetary policy and financial stability, mainly through its Monetary Policy Committee and Financial Policy Committee; primary operational responsibility for financial crisis management; oversight of payment systems, settlement systems and central counterparties; resolution authority; and, as the PRA, prudential regulation ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=1]], [[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=2]]).
- **Resolution authority.** The Bank is the UK's resolution authority: it sets the preferred resolution strategy for every firm and works with firms to remove impediments to orderly resolution ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=11]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=37]]). See [[wiki/concepts/Resolvability]].
- **Independence inside the Bank.** The [[wiki/entities/Prudential Regulation Committee]] reports each year to the Chancellor on how far the PRA's functions are independent of the Bank's other functions ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=15]]).
- **Liquidity facilities.** The Bank offers liquidity insurance (ILTR auctions, the Discount Window Facility, the Contingent Term Repo Facility), meant neither for routine use nor only as a last resort ([[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=35]], [[raw/pra-approach-banking-supervision-2023.pdf#page=36]]).
