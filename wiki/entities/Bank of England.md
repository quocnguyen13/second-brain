# Bank of England

Acting through its Prudential Regulation Committee, the Bank of England is the Prudential Regulation Authority. In its other capacity it pursues a Financial Stability Objective ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], ss. 2A, 3Q).

## What the sources say
- **It is the PRA.** The "Prudential Regulation Authority" is the Bank of England. Its PRA functions are exercisable only through the Prudential Regulation Committee ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2A(1)–(2)).
- **Two capacities kept apart.** In FSMA, "the Bank of England" normally excludes the Bank acting as the PRA, and "the PRA" excludes the Bank acting in any other capacity ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2A(3)–(4)).
- **Strategy.** The PRA must consult the Bank's court of directors on its strategy ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 2E(2)).
- **Co-operation with the FCA.** The FCA must co-operate with the Bank on the Bank's Financial Stability Objective, which can include sharing information ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3Q). The FCA and the Bank may provide services to each other ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3R(2)).
- **Financial Policy Committee.** Rules made to comply with a direction or recommendation of the Bank's Financial Policy Committee are outside the regulators' general duty to review rules ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-part-1a.md]], s. 3RA(2)).
