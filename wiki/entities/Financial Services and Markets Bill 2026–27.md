# Financial Services and Markets Bill 2026–27

> [!warning] AI only: no source in raw/ backs this page yet.

A government Bill, introduced in the House of Lords in May 2026, that would reform UK financial services regulation, including the PSR, the Financial Ombudsman Service, authorisation and ring-fencing ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).

## What the sources say

### Stages (as of 2026-09-27)
- First reading in the Lords on 19 May 2026 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Second reading on 8 June 2026 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Committee stage from 22 June to 8 July 2026 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Report stage ended on 9 September 2026 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Brought from the Lords to the Commons as Bill 152 of 2026–27, dated 15 September 2026 ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Not yet law ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).

### Scope
- It would move the [[wiki/entities/Payment Systems Regulator]] into the FCA ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- The PSR stays in place until the Act is passed and commenced ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- It reforms the [[wiki/entities/Financial Ombudsman Service]] ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- It introduces a "provisional licences" authorisation scheme ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI). See [[wiki/concepts/New bank authorisation]] for the current process.
- It reforms [[wiki/concepts/Ring-fencing]] ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- The Lords removed a clause that would have let the Treasury amend access-to-banking legislation ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
- Its provisions draw on the government's growth and competitiveness strategy and its regulation action plan ([[raw/claude-2026-09-27-foundations-1-1-who-regulates-what.md]] · AI).
