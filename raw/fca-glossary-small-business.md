(1) (in [COMP](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G189) and in the definition of [relevant credit union client](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3553r) ) a [partnership](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G843), [body corporate](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G111), unincorporated association or mutual association with an annual turnover of less than £1 million (or its equivalent in any other currency at the relevant time).

(2) (in [DISP](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G302) and in the definition of [larger commercial customer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/Gfb6644ddc18804e3acee946d88e793ca)) an [enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G93516e) which:

1. (a) is not a [micro-enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2623);
2. (b) has an annual turnover of less than £6.5 million (or its equivalent in any other currency); and
	1. (i) employs fewer than 50 [persons](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869); or
		2. (ii) has a balance sheet total of less than £5 million (or its equivalent in any other currency),
	and in determining whether these criteria are met articles 3 to 6 of the Annex to the [Micro-enterprise Recommendation](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2625) must be applied.
