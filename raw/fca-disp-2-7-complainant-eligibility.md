## DISP 2.7 Is the complainant eligible?

A [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) may only be dealt with under the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419) if it is brought by or on behalf of an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349).

A [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) may be brought on behalf of an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) (or a deceased [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) who would have been an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349)) by a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) authorised by the [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) or authorised by law. It is immaterial whether the [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) authorised to act on behalf of an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) is himself an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349).

### Eligible complainants

An [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) must be a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) that is:

1. (1)
	a [consumer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G210); or
2. (2)
	a [micro-enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2623);
	1. (a)
		in relation to a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) relating wholly or partly to [payment services](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2617), either at the time of the conclusion of the [payment service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2617) contract or at the time the complainant refers the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
		2. (b)
		otherwise, at the time the complainant refers the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
3. (3)
	a charity which has an annual income of less than £6.5 million at the time the complainant refers the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
4. (4)
	a trustee of a trust which has a net asset value of less than £5 million at the time the complainant refers the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
5. (5)
	(in relation to [CBTL business](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3545c)) a [CBTL consumer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3546c); or
6. (6)
	a [small business](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1095) at the time the complainant refers the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
7. (7)
	a [guarantor](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1771).

In determining whether an [enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G93516e) meets the tests for being a [micro-enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2623) or a [small business](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1095), account should be taken of the [enterprise’s](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G93516e) 'partner enterprises' or 'linked enterprises' (as those terms are defined in the [Micro-enterprise Recommendation](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2625)). For example, where a parent company holds a majority shareholding in a [complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), if the parent company does not meet the tests for being a [micro-enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2623) or a [small business](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1095) then neither will the [complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197).

\[**Note**: articles 1 and 3 to 6 of the Annex to the [Micro-enterprise Recommendation](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2625)\].

If a [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is in doubt about the eligibility of a business, charity or trust, it should treat the complainant as if it were eligible. If the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is referred to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419), the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) will determine eligibility by reference to appropriate evidence, such as audited accounts or VAT returns.

A [guarantor](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1771) shall be an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) only to the extent that their [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) arises from matters relevant to the relationship with the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) referred to in [DISP 2.7.6R(10)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341).

To be an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) must also have a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) which arises from matters relevant to one or more of the following relationships with the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497):

1. (1)
	the complainant is (or was) a customer,[payment service user](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2620) or electronic money holder of the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);
2. (2)
	the complainant is (or was) a potential customer,[payment service user](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2620) or electronic money holder of the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);
3. (2A)
	the complainant is (or was) a [payer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3487p) in a [payment transaction](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3490p) in relation to which the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is (or was) the [payee’s](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3486p) [payment service provider](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2619), provided the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) relates to the [respondent’s](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) obligations under regulation 90(3) of the [Payment Services Regulations](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2621);
4. (2B)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) that has transferred funds as a result of an alleged [authorised push payment fraud](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3566a) and both:
	1. (a)
		the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is (or was) involved in the transfer of the funds; and
		2. (b)
		the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is not a [PSD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3512p);
5. (3)
	the complainant is the holder, or the beneficial owner, of [units](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1230) in a [collective investment scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G178) and the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is:
	1. (a)
		the [operator](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G803) of a [scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1043); or
		2. (b)
		the [depositary](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G278) of an [authorised fund](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G86); or
		3. (c)
		the [depositary](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G278) of a [charity AIF](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3107); or
		4. (d)
		\[deleted\]
6. (3A)
	the complainant is the holder, or the beneficial owner, of [units](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1230) or [shares](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1078) in an [AIF](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3090) that is not a [collective investment scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G178) where the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is:
	1. (a)
		the [AIFM](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3092) of an [unauthorised AIF](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3148) (other than a [body corporate](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G111)); or
		2. (b)
		\[deleted\]
		3. (c)
		the [AIFM](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3092) or [depositary](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G278) of a [charity AIF](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3107) (other than a [body corporate](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G111));
7. (4)
	the complainant is a beneficiary of, or has a beneficial interest in, a [personal pension scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G876) or [stakeholder pension scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1124);
8. (5)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) for whose benefit a [contract of insurance](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G218) was taken out or was intended to be taken out with or through the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);
9. (6)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) on whom the legal right to benefit from a claim against the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) under a [contract of insurance](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G218) has been devolved by contract, assignment, subrogation or legislation (save the European Community (Rights against Insurers) Regulations 2002);
10. (7)
	the complainant relied in the course of his business on a cheque guarantee card issued by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);
11. (8)
	the complainant is the true owner or the [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) entitled to immediate possession of a cheque or other bill of exchange, or of the funds it represents, collected by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) for someone else's account;
12. (9)
	the complainant is the recipient of a banker's reference given by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);
13. (10)
	the complainant gave the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) a guarantee or security for:
	1. (a)

		3. (c)
		an actual or prospective [regulated credit agreement](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3184);
		4. (d)
		an actual or prospective [regulated consumer hire agreement](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1989); or
		5. (e)
		any linked transaction as defined in the [Consumer Credit Act 1974](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1974/39/2008-04-06) (as amended);
14. (11)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) about whom information relevant to his financial standing is or was held by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) in [providing credit references](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3217);
15. (11A)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) about whom information relevant to his financial standing is or was held by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) in providing [credit information](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3564c);
16. (11B)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) about whom [specified information](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G4572s) was provided to a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) in relation to a [finance application](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3511f);
17. (12)
	the complainant is a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869):
	1. (a)
		from whom the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) has sought to recover payment under a [credit agreement](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3163) or [consumer hire agreement](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3162) (whether or not the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is a party to the agreement); or
		2. (b)
		in relation to whom the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) has sought to perform duties, or exercise or enforce rights, on behalf of the creditor or owner, under a [credit agreement](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3163) or [consumer hire agreement](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3162) in carrying on [debt administration](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3203);
18. (13)
	the complainant is a beneficiary under a trust or estate of which the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is trustee or personal representative;
19. (14)
	(where the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is a [dormant asset fund operator](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3582d)) the complainant is (or was) a customer of a [bank](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G97) or [building society](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G118) which transferred any [balance](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2658) from a [dormant asset](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3580d) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);
20. (14A)
	(where the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is a [dormant asset fund operator](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3582d)) subject to [DISP 2.7.6AR(1)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p42615), the complainant is (or was) a [person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869) to whom the proceeds of a [long-term insurance contract](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G673) were payable by an [insurer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G569), but which instead were transferred by the [insurer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G569) to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497);

22. (14C)

25. (16)
	the complainant is a [client](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G156) (where the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) is an [ISPV](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1941));
26. (17)
	the complainant is a [customer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G252) of the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) in relation to [regulated claims management activity](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3567r);
27. (18)
	the complainant is a [customer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G252) of the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) in relation to [regulated pensions dashboard activity](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3618r).

1. (1)
	[DISP 2.7.6R (14A)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) does not include proceeds of a [contract of insurance](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G218) held in a [lifetime ISA](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3394l) if their transfer to a [dormant asset fund operator](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3582d) resulted in (or would result in) liability to pay a [lifetime ISA government withdrawal charge](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3398l).
2. (2)
	[DISP 2.7.6R(14D)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) does not include [client money](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G160) held in a [lifetime ISA](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3394l) if its transfer to a [dormant asset fund operator](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3582d) resulted in (or would result in) liability to pay a [lifetime ISA government withdrawal charge](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3398l).

Benefits under a [personal pension scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G876) in [DISP 2.7.6R(14B)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) are excluded from being ‘eligible pension benefits’ if (or to the extent that) they are provided from sums invested in a [with-profits fund](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1260).

1. (1) [*DISP 2.7.6R (5)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) and [*DISP 2.7.6R (6)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) include, for example, employees covered by a group permanent health policy taken out by an employer, which provides in the insurance contract that the policy was taken out for the benefit of the employee.
2. (2) [DISP 2.7.6R(2B)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) includes any [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) that the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) did not do enough to prevent, or respond to, an alleged [authorised push payment fraud](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3566a).
3. (3) [*DISP* 2.7.6R(4)](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s7#p11341) includes a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) where the complainant has an indirect relationship with the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) which arises from the complainant’s circumstances as described in this [rule](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1036). For example, a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) by a beneficiary of a [personal pension scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G876) arising from the [respondent's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) obligations to the trustee or to the [operator](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G803) of that scheme where the performance of those obligations impacts on the rights, interests, or situation of the complainant as a beneficiary of that pension scheme.

In addition, an individual is an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) if:

1. (1)
	they have been identified by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) as a [politically exposed person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3516p), a [family member of a politically exposed person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3518f), or a [known close associate of a politically exposed person](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2937k); and
2. (2)
	their [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197):
	1. (a)
		is that such identification is incorrect; or
		2. (b)
		relates to an act or omission by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) in consequence of such identification.

In the [Compulsory Jurisdiction](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G202), under the [Ombudsman Transitional Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G795), the [Mortgages and General Insurance Complaints Transitional Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1446), [Claims Management Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3592c) and the [Funeral Plans Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3535f), where a complainant:

1. (1)
	wishes to have a [relevant new complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1004), a [relevant transitional complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1448) a [relevant new claims management complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3570r), or a [relevant transitional funeral plan complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3588r) dealt with by the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794); and
2. (2)
	is not otherwise eligible; but
3. (3)
	would have been entitled to refer an equivalent [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [former scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G438) in question immediately before the relevant order came into effect;

if the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) considers it appropriate, he may treat the complainant as an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349).

### Exceptions

The following are not [eligible complainants](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349):

1. (1)
	(in all jurisdictions) a [firm](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G430), [payment service provider](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2619), [electronic money issuer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2841), [CBTL firm](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3549c), [designated credit reference agency](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3550d), [designated finance platform](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3555d) or [VJ participant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1247) whose [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) relates in any way to an activity which:
	1. (a)
		the [firm](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G430) itself has [permission](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G863) to carry on; or
		2. (ab)
		the [firm](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G430), [payment service provider](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2619), [electronic money issuer](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2841), [CBTL firm](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3549c), [designated credit reference agency](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3550d) or [designated finance platform](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3555d) itself is entitled to carry on under the [Payment Services Regulations](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2621), the [Electronic Money Regulations](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2842), the [MCD Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3529m), the [Small and Medium Sized Business (Credit Information) Regulations](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G4566s) or the [Small and Medium Sized Business (Finance Platforms) Regulations](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G4571s); or
		3. (b)
		the [VJ participant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1247) itself conducts;

and which is subject to the [Compulsory Jurisdiction](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G202) or the [Voluntary Jurisdiction](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1248);

In the [Compulsory Jurisdiction](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G202), in relation to [relevant new complaints](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1004) under the [Ombudsman Transitional Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G795) and [relevant transitional complaints](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1448) under the [Mortgages and General Insurance Complaints Transitional Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1446):

1. (1)
	where the [former scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G438) in question is the [Insurance Ombudsman Scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G567), a complainant is not to be treated as an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) unless:
	1. (a)
		he is an individual; and
		2. (b)
		the [relevant new complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1004) does not concern aspects of a policy relating to a business or trade carried on by him;
2. (2)
	where the [former scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G438) in question is the [GISC facility](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1444), a complainant is not to be treated as an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) unless:
	1. (a)
		he is an individual; and
		2. (b)
		he is acting otherwise than solely for the purposes of his business; and
3. (3)
	where the [former scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G438) in question is the [MCAS scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1445), a complainant is not to be treated as an [eligible complainant](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G349) if:
	1. (a)
		the [relevant transitional complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1448) does not relate to a breach of the Mortgage Code published by the Council of Mortgage Lenders;
		2. (b)
		the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) concerns physical injury, illness, nervous shock or their consequences; or
		3. (c)
		the complainant is claiming a sum of money that exceeds £100,000.
