an [enterprise](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G93516e) which:

1. (a) employs fewer than 10 [persons](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G869); and
1. (b) has a turnover or annual balance sheet that does not exceed €2 million,

and in determining whether these criteria are met articles 3 to 6 of the Annex to the [Micro-enterprise Recommendation](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2625) must be applied.

\[Note: article 4(36 ) of the [Payment Services Directive](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2618) and the Annex to the [Micro-enterprise Recommendation](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2625)\]
