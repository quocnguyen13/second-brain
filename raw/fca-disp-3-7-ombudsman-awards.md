## DISP 3.7 Awards by the Ombudsman

You are viewing DISP 3.7 Awards by the Ombudsman as of 14/10/2026. DISP 3.7 Awards by the Ombudsman was last updated on 26/03/2026.   

## DISP 3.7 Awards by the Ombudsman

Where a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is determined in favour of the complainant, the [Ombudsman's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) determination may include one or more of the following:

1. (1)
	a money award against the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
2. (2)
	an interest award against the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
3. (3)
	a costs award against the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497); or
4. (4)
	a direction to the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497).

### Money awards

Except in relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10), a money award may be such amount as the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) considers to be fair compensation for one or more of the following:

1. (1)
	financial loss (including consequential or prospective loss); or
2. (2)
	pain and suffering; or
3. (3)
	damage to reputation; or
4. (4)
	distress or inconvenience;

whether or not a court would award compensation.

In relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10), a money award is a payment of such amount as the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) determines that a [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) should make (or should have made) to a complainant under the scheme.

A money award under [DISP 3.7.2A G](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp3/disp3s7#p27309) may specify the date by which the amount awarded is to be paid.

1. (1)
	Where the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) is determining what amount (if any) constitutes fair compensation as a money award in relation to a [relevant new complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1004), a [relevant transitional complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1448), or a [relevant new claims management complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3570r), the [Ombudsman Transitional Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G795), the [Mortgages and General Insurance Complaints Transitional Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1446), and the [Claims Management Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3592c) make provision for the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) to take into account what amount (if any) might have been expected to be awarded by way of compensation in relation to an equivalent complaint dealt with under the [former scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G438) in question immediately before the relevant order came into effect.
2. (2)
	Where the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) is determining what amount (if any) constitutes fair compensation as a money award in relation to a [relevant transitional funeral plan complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3588r), the [Funeral Plans Order](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3535f) makes provision for the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) to take into account what amount, if any, the [Funeral Planning Authority](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3532f) might have been expected to award by way of compensation in relation to an equivalent complaint dealt with under the [former scheme](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G438).

1. (1)
	The maximum money award which the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) may make is:
	1. (a)
		£350,000 for a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) concerning an act or omission which occurred on or after 1 April 2019; and
		2. (b)
		£160,000 for a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) concerning an act or omission which occurred before 1 April 2019.
2. (2)
	On 1 April each year, for [complaints](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) referred to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419) on or after this date up to and including 31 March in the following year, the amounts in (1)(a) and (b) are adjusted by:
	1. (a)
		applying the percentage increase in [CPI](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2962) between January 2019 and January of that year; and
		2. (b)
		rounding down to the nearest £5,000.

The effect of [section 404B(5)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2011-08-01#section-404B-5) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10) is that the maximum award which the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) may make also applies in relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10).

For the purpose of calculating the maximum money award, the following are excluded:

1. (1)
	any interest awarded on the amount payable under a money award;
2. (2)
	any costs awarded; and
3. (3)
	any interest awarded on costs.

If the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) considers that fair compensation requires payment of a larger amount, he may recommend that the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) pays the complainant the balance. The effect of [section 404B(6)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2011-08-01#section-404B-6) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10) is that this is also the case in relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10).

### Interest awards

Except in relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10), an interest award may provide for the amount payable under the money award to bear interest at a rate and as from a date specified in the award.

A money award under [DISP 3.7.2A G](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp3/disp3s7#p27309) may provide for interest to be payable, at a rate specified in the award, on any amount which is not paid by the date specified in the award.

### Costs awards

A costs award may:

1. (1)
	be such amount as the Ombudsman considers to be fair, to cover some or all of the costs which were reasonably incurred by the complainant in respect of the complaint; and
2. (2)
	include interest on that amount at a rate and as from a date specified in the award.

In most cases complainants should not need to have professional advisers to bring [complaints](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419), so awards of costs are unlikely to be common.

### Directions

Except in relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10), a direction may require the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) to take such steps in relation to the complainant as the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) considers just and appropriate (whether or not a court could order those steps to be taken).

In relation to a “relevant complaint” within the meaning of [section 404B(3)](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2000/8/section/404B/2015-07-31#section-404B-3) of the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10), a direction may require the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) to take such action as the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) determines the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) should take (or should have taken) under the scheme.

### Complying with awards and settlements

A [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) must comply promptly with:

1. (1)
	any award or direction made by the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794); and
2. (2)
	any settlement which it agrees at an earlier stage of the procedures.

Under the [Act](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G10), a complainant can enforce through the courts a money award registered by the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794) or a direction made by the [Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G794).
