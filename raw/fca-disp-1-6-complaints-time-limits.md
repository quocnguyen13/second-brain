## DISP 1.6 Complaints time limit rules

## DISP 1.6 Complaints time limit rules

### Keeping the complainant informed

On receipt of a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), a [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) must:

1. (1) send the complainant a prompt written acknowledgement providing:
	1. (a) early reassurance that it has received the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) and is dealing with it; and
		2. (b) a statement explaining that the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) will send:
		1. (i) in the case of an [EMD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3519e) or a [PSD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3512p):
			1. (A) a final response within 15 [business days](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G120) of its receipt of the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), in accordance with [*DISP 1.6.2AR(1)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319); or
						2. (B) in exceptional circumstances, a holding response within 15 [business days](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G120) of its receipt of the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) and a final response within 35 [business days](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G120) of its receipt of the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), in accordance with [*DISP 1.6.2AR(2)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319); or
				2. (ii) in the case of any other [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), a written response within 8 weeks of its receipt of the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), that being either a final response in accordance with [*DISP 1.6.2R(1)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11268) or a written response in accordance with [*DISP 1.6.2R(2)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11268); and
2. (2)
	ensure the complainant is kept informed thereafter of the progress of the measures being taken for the [complaint's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) resolution.

To the extent that a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is in part an [EMD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3519e) or a [PSD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3512p) and the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) has chosen to deal with it in parts, keeping the complainant informed of progress includes informing the complainant that this is the approach that the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) will take.

Where the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is due to be dealt with through the [Lloyd’s complaint procedures](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2495), and consistently with [*DISP 1.1.7R*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s1#p11200), the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) will be able to satisfy the requirement at [*DISP 1.6.1R(1)(b)*](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11267) by including information about the timelines applicable under the [Lloyd’s complaint procedures](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2495).

### Final or other response within eight weeks

Subject to [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319), the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) must, by the end of eight weeks after its receipt of the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), send the complainant:

1. (1)
	a 'final response', being a written response from the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) which:
	1. (a)
		accepts the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) and, where appropriate, offers redress or remedial action; or
		2. (b)
		offers redress or remedial action without accepting the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197); or
		3. (c)
		rejects the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) and gives reasons for doing so;
	and which:
	1. (d)
		encloses a copy of the [Financial Ombudsman Service's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419) standard explanatory leaflet;
		2. (da)
		provides the website address of the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419);
		3. (e)
		informs the complainant that if he remains dissatisfied with the [respondent's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) response, he may now refer his [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419); and
		4. (f)
		indicates whether or not the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) consents to waive the relevant time limits in [DISP 2.8.2 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p25179) or [DISP 2.8.7 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p25182) (Was the complaint referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service in time?) by including the appropriate wording set out in [DISP 1 Annex 3R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s18#p33198); or
	\[**Note:** [respondents](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) are reminded of their obligations under regulation 19 of the [ADR Regulations](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3541), which requires [respondents](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) to provide equivalent messaging in respect of the time limit in [DISP 2.8.9R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p36050) (Payment protection insurance complaints)\]
2. (2)
	a written response which:
	1. (a)
		explains why it is not in a position to make a [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411) and indicates when it expects to be able to provide one;
		2. (b)
		informs the complainant that he may now refer the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419);
		3. (ba)
		indicates whether or not the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) consents to waive the relevant time limits in [DISP 2.8.2 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p25179) or [DISP 2.8.7 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p25182) (Was the complaint referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service in time?) if it becomes apparent that the complaint has been made or is referred outside those time limits;
		4. (c)
		encloses a copy of the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419) standard explanatory leaflet; and
		5. (d)
		provides the website address of the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419).

\[**Note:** article 13 of the [ADR Directive](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3539)\]

### EMD and PSD Complaints

Where a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is an [EMD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3519e) or a [PSD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3512p), the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) must:

1. (1)
	send a [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411) to the complainant by the end of 15 [business days](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G120) after the day on which it received the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197); or
2. (2)
	in exceptional circumstances, if a [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411) cannot be given in accordance with paragraph (1) for reasons beyond the control of the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497):
	1. (a)
		send a holding response to the complainant by the end of 15 [business days](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G120) after the day on which it received the complaint, clearly indicating the reasons for the delay in answering the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) and specifying the deadline by which it will send the [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411); and
		2. (b)
		send a [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411) to the complainant by the end of 35 [business days](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G120) after the day on which it received the [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197).
	A [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411) sent under (1) or (2) above must be on paper, or if agreed between the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) and the complainant, on another [durable medium](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1286).

\[**Note:** article 101 of the [Payment Services Directive](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2618)\]

Where only part of a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is an [EMD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3519e) or a [PSD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3512p), that part must be treated in accordance with [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319).

As the time limits in [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319) are shorter than those in [DISP 1.6.2R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11268) a [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) may choose to treat the whole [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) in accordance with [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319) (see also [DISP 2.8AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8)).

### Complainant's written acceptance

[DISP 1.6.2 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11268) does not apply if the complainant has already indicated in writing acceptance of a response by the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497), provided that the response:

1. (1)
	informed the complainant how to pursue his [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) with the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) if he remains dissatisfied;
2. (2)
	referred to the ultimate availability of the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419) if he remains dissatisfied with the [respondent's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) response;
3. (3)
	enclosed a copy of the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419) standard explanatory leaflet;
4. (4)
	provided the website address of the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419); and
5. (5)
	indicated whether or not the [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) consents to waive the relevant time limits in [DISP 2.8.2 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p25179) or [DISP 2.8.7 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp2/disp2s8#p25182) (Was the complaint referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service in time?) by including the appropriate wording set out in [DISP 1 Annex 3 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s18#p33198).

[DISP 1.6.4R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p24365) does not affect the requirements imposed by [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319). Where a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) is an [EMD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3519e) or a [PSD complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3512p) and [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319) applies a final response must always be sent unless [DISP 1.5.1R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s5#p11256) applies.

The information regarding the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419), required to be provided in responses sent under the [complaints](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) time limit [rules](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G1036) ([DISP 1.6.2 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11268), [DISP 1.6.2AR](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p37319) and [DISP 1.6.4 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p24365)), should be set out clearly, comprehensibly, in an easily accessible way and prominently within the text of those responses.

\[**Note:** article 13 of the [ADR Directive](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3539)\]

A [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) may, where relevant, in a response sent under the [complaints time limits rules](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2494) ([DISP 1.6.2R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p11268) and [DISP 1.6.4R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p24365)) refer to the availability of the [Pensions Ombudsman](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G3515p), in addition to the [Financial Ombudsman Service](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G419), by including the wording set out in [DISP 1 Annex 4G](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s19#p36820).

### Speed and quality of response

It is expected that within eight weeks of their receipt, almost all [complaints](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197) to a [respondent](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) will have been substantively addressed by it through a [final response](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G411) or response as described in [DISP 1.6.4 R](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/disp1/disp1s6#p24365).

When assessing a [respondent's](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2497) response to a [complaint](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G197), the [FCA](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2974) may have regard to a number of factors, including, the quality of response, as against the [complaints resolution rules](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/glossary/G2492), as well as the speed with which it was made.
