## Conflicts
1. **scope** · [[wiki/concepts/FCA-PRA coordination]] · when the PRA needs the FCA's consent, not only consultation, to vary or cancel a permission
   - A: On a firm's application, the PRA needs the FCA's consent to add, remove or re-describe an activity, and consults it before cancelling ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=89]], s. 55I(1)–(2)). On the PRA's own initiative, it needs consent only to add an activity or widen a description, and otherwise consults ([[raw/uk-parliament-fsma-2000-2026-09.pdf#page=91]], s. 55J(5)) · primary · up to date to 2026-09-10
   - B: "As provided for in FSMA", the PRA obtains the FCA's consent before granting or removing a permission or adding an activity, and consults it before cancelling ([[raw/fca-boe-memorandum-of-understanding-2024.pdf#page=21]], Annex 1 para 1) · primary · 2024-03-26
   - **Proposal: c**, scoped. The MoU paragraph sits under "Applications to grant, vary or cancel a regulatory permission", so it describes the application route, where it matches the Act.
   - Options: **1a** state A, mark B · **1b** state B, mark A · **1c** both hold: "On a firm's application, the PRA needs the FCA's consent to grant, add, remove or re-describe, and consults it before cancelling (Act s. 55I; MoU Annex 1 para 1). On the PRA's own initiative, consent is needed only to add an activity or widen a description; removing or cancelling needs consultation (s. 55J(5), (9))." · **1d** leave open
   → Resolved 2026-10-20: c
